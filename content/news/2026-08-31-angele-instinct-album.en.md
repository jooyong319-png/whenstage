Angèle has announced Instinct, arriving October 16th via Angèle VL Records and Sony Music, with guest turns from Justice, Soulwax and Caroline Polachek.

She previewed it with "Une seule vie," produced with Tristan Salvati and paired with a 16mm video directed by Thomas Mailaender.

The single follows June's "Dis-Le" and the Justice collaboration "What You Want." Pre-orders are open now.
