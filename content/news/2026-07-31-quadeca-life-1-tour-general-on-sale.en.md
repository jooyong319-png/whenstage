Following an artist presale earlier this week, tickets for Quadeca's "The Life 1 Tour" open to the general public Friday, July 31st at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster.

The 23-date North American run begins October 10th in Mesa, Arizona and ends November 10th at the Fonda in his native Los Angeles, with Derby and Oleka opening. A portion of proceeds goes to City of Hope.

He plays Lollapalooza on August 1st first.
