A severe storm tore through southern Ontario on September 2nd, leaving what Live Nation Canada called significant damage at Rogers Stadium. No one was hurt, and the company says it is still measuring how much repair work is needed.

AC/DC's Power Up Tour stop on September 16th is the only show left on the venue's 2026 calendar, and neither the band nor the promoter has announced a change.

Anyone holding tickets should keep an eye on official channels this week.
