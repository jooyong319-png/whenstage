Tony Iommi has announced *From the Dark*, arriving October 23rd via BMG. It is his first solo record in 21 years, following 2005's *Fused* with Glenn Hughes, and was co-produced with longtime collaborator Mike Exeter.

Norwegian vocalist Jorn Lande sings and co-wrote every track, backed by bassist Becky Baldwin and drummer Karl Brazil. Lead single "World Alone" arrived with a video, and pre-orders are open across CD and multiple vinyl variants.
