Demand for the Dublin band's first Slane booking was heavy enough that they have added a [second night at the County Meath estate](/en/concert/en-fontaines-dc-slane-castle-20270627) on Sunday, June 27th, 2027, again in front of 80,000 people.

The band confirmed the extra date on September 2nd. Presale access opened the same morning, with the general on-sale following at 10am on September 3rd.

Both weekend dates fall over Glastonbury. See full details on the [event page](/en/concert/en-fontaines-dc-slane-castle-20270627).
