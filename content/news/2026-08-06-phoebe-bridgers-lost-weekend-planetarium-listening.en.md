Ahead of Lost Weekend's August 14th release on Dead Oceans, Phoebe Bridgers is previewing the album inside more than 40 planetariums, beginning Monday, August 10th. Sessions are phone-free and pair the record with either a Babak Tafreshi dome film or a Laser Fantasy light show.

Participating domes include Chicago's Adler, San Francisco's Morrison and Melbourne Planetarium. Over 300 record stores host midnight parties on the 13th.

Her [Lost Tour](/en/concert/en-phoebe-bridgers-lost-tour-indianapolis-20260914) follows, though US and UK dates are already sold out.
