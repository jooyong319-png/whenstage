Beyond The Music has announced its second wave of artists for the 2026 edition, spread across venues in MediaCity, Salford from October 6th to 9th.

Tom A Smith, Gans and The Slow Readers Club lead the additions, joined by Walt Disco, Moth Ulcers, Cody Frost, ALT BLK ERA and JD Cliffe, with more names to be confirmed.

The week opens with a 24th anniversary screening of 24 Hour Party People and closes with an AI summit convened by Andy Burnham.
