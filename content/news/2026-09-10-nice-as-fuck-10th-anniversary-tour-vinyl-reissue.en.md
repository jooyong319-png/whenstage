Jenny Lewis, Erika Spring and Tennessee Thomas are reviving Nice as Fuck for eight US club shows marking ten years since their self-titled debut.

The run opens October 16th in Pasadena and passes through San Francisco, Seattle, Portland, Chicago, Washington DC and Philadelphia before closing October 29th at Brooklyn's Xanadu Roller Arts, with DJ Alix Brown supporting throughout.

The long out-of-print album returns on vinyl, sold at the shows or by preorder. Tickets are on sale now.
