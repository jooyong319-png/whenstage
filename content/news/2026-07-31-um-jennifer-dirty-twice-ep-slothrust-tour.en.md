Brooklyn post-punk pair Um, Jennifer? have dated their new EP *Dirty Twice* for August 21st via Final Girl, with pre-orders live now. Lead single "Bushwick Ratbag" arrived alongside a video shot at a local bar.

Fig and Eli wrote and recorded the five-track set themselves, with Teddy O'Mara producing. February's "Comedy 42" also made the cut.

They follow it with 16 fall dates supporting Slothrust, running October 27th in San Francisco through November 18th at The Bellwether in Los Angeles.
