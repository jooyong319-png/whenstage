Erykah Badu and The Alchemist have dated their long-teased collaborative album for August 28th, sharing lead single "Witch Doctor" alongside the announcement. Pre-orders are live.

Recorded across Dallas and Los Angeles over 18 months, the 17-track set is listed on streaming platforms under the placeholder title *Sorry, we don't have an album title yet …*, and moves between soul, blues, funk and underground hip-hop.

The pair tour North America with De La Soul from September 10th, closing at the Hollywood Bowl September 29th.
