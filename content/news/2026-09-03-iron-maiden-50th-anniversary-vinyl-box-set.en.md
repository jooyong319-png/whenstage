The band have detailed 50th Anniversary Studio Album Collection, Part One: 1980 – 1992, gathering their first nine studio albums on individually coloured vinyl using the 2015 remasters. It lands December 11th.

A standalone 40th anniversary pressing of Somewhere in Time, on oxblood vinyl, comes first on December 4th. Amazon carries poster variants of both.

The nine-album span mirrors the setlist of the "Run For Your Lives" tour now crossing North America with Megadeth and Anthrax.
