Garth Brooks has confirmed the Chicago leg of his "Blame It All On My Roots" arena tour: four nights at Allstate Arena in Rosemont, September 10th through 13th, closing with a Sunday matinee.

Tickets are already on sale. Every seat carries the same all-in price of $155.25, covering a $140 face value plus fees. The run revives Brooks' in-the-round Drum Pod stage.
