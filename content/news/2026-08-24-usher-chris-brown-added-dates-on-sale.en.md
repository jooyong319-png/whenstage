General tickets for the eight stadium dates Usher and Chris Brown added to The R&B Tour went on sale Monday, August 24th at 10am local time through Ticketmaster and RaymondAndBrownTour.com.

The additions take the co-headlining trek to 59 shows and bring first stops in Baltimore, Cincinnati, Columbia and Indianapolis, plus extra nights in East Rutherford, Los Angeles, Atlanta and Miami Gardens.

VIP packages are being sold separately through VIP Nation.
