Dolly Parton's estate has announced DOLLYFEST: The Celebration of a Lifetime, running across consecutive weekends in Nashville and London during 2027. Dates, venues, lineup and ticketing have not been set.

Manager Danny Nozell said the event was already in planning around Parton's 80th birthday before her health forced her to step away in 2025.

Her family held a private funeral, and no traditional public memorial is planned.
