General tickets for Noah Kahan's first "The Last Of The Bugs World Tour" dates open Friday, August 28th at 10am local time, following an artist presale on August 25th.

The three July 2027 stadium shows are Glasgow's Hampden Park on the 13th, Dublin's Aviva Stadium on the 17th and London's Wembley Stadium on the 23rd.

Kahan named the run after the expanded edition of his fourth album, The Great Divide, and has said more dates are still to come.
