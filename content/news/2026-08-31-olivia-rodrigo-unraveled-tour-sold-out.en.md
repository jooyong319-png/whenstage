Olivia Rodrigo's [The Unraveled Tour](/en/concert/en-olivia-rodrigo-unraveled-tour-philadelphia-20261107) is sold out ahead of its September 25th opener in Hartford, Connecticut. The run covers 98 shows across North America, Europe and the UK.

A fresh round of $25 Silver Star tickets is the main remaining route in, alongside verified resale.

It follows her Daisy Chain Fields charity festival last weekend. See full details on the [event page](/en/concert/en-olivia-rodrigo-unraveled-tour-philadelphia-20261107).
