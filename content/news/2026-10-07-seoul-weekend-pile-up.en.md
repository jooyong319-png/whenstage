Scroll our October calendar and one thing jumps out that no single listing shows you: this week the busiest city on our calendar is Seoul. Not one marquee stadium night — six separate runs stacked onto the same three days, plus a festival an hour out of town.

The weekend of the 9th to the 11th is where it piles up. [AKMU's Paradise of Rumors](/en/concert/en-akmu-paradise-of-rumors-seoul-20261009) takes KSPO Dome for three nights, each with a different curtain: 6pm on the 9th, [5pm on the 10th](/en/concert/en-akmu-paradise-of-rumors-seoul-20261010), [4pm on the 11th](/en/concert/en-akmu-paradise-of-rumors-seoul-20261011) — Sunday's early start is built for fans flying out of Incheon that night. Across the same Olympic Park, on the same three evenings, [10CM's THE MISSING TRACKS](/en/concert/en-10cm-the-missing-tracks-seoul-20261009) runs at Olympic Hall at 6pm, each night subtitled as its own case number. Two completely different crowds, one park, one set of subway exits.

Elsewhere in the city the clashes are sharper. [tripleS open their ANDLESS world tour](/en/concert/en-triples-andless-seoul-20261010) at Jangchung Arena on the 10th and [close Seoul on the 11th](/en/concert/en-triples-andless-seoul-20261011) before the run heads to Macau, Tokyo and North America; S-section seats are 30,000 won, the cheapest arena ticket of the weekend. [PENTAGON's 101010](/en/concert/en-pentagon-101010-seoul-20261009) hits Blue Square on the 9th and 10th, the latter the exact date of their 2016 debut; both nights sold out when booking opened. [ALL(H)OURS start their first world tour](/en/concert/en-allhours-rise-up-seoul-20261010) at 5pm on the 10th.

If you want air, the [Jarasum International Jazz Festival](/en/concert/en-jarasum-jazz-festival-2026-20261009) runs all three days on an island in Gapyeong, an hour east by ITX.

What to grab first depends on the 11th, the genuine traffic jam: AKMU at 4pm, tripleS, Jarasum's final day, and [Charlie Puth's first Korean stadium show](/en/concert/en-charlie-puth-whatevers-clever-seoul-20261011) at Goyang Stadium at 7pm. The AKMU matinee and Puth are the only pair that actually fit back to back.

The rest of the week is thinner by comparison, and worth knowing for that reason. [The Weeknd plays Goyang Stadium tonight](/en/concert/en-the-weeknd-after-hours-til-dawn-seoul-20261007) with Creepy Nuts opening, 19+. Far from Korea, [Shakira ends her twelve-night Madrid residency](/en/concert/en-shakira-las-mujeres-ya-no-lloran-madrid-20261011) on the 11th, and [Beyond The Music](/en/concert/en-beyond-the-music-2026-salford-20261007) opens three days at MediaCity in Salford today. Otherwise it is a theatre-and-club week: [Wynonna Judd at Bowery Ballroom](/en/concert/en-wynonna-judd-hard-truth-new-york-20261008) on the 8th, [Steve Lacy at Radio City](/en/concert/en-steve-lacy-radio-city-20261010) on the 10th, and [The Pussycat Dolls at The O2](/en/concert/en-pussycat-dolls-pcd-forever-london-20261013) on the 13th.
