Pentatonix have laid out 28 North American arena dates for the 2026 edition of [Christmas in the City](/en/concert/en-pentatonix-christmas-in-the-city-tacoma-20261105), opening November 5th in Tacoma and closing December 22nd in Los Angeles, by way of Vancouver, Nashville, Atlanta and Austin.

Presales run August 10th through 12th for Patreon members, artist signups and Live Nation. General sale is Friday, August 14th at 10 a.m. local.

New single "Get Happy / Happy Days Are Here Again" previews an October 30th deluxe reissue. Details on the [event page](/en/concert/en-pentatonix-christmas-in-the-city-tacoma-20261105).
