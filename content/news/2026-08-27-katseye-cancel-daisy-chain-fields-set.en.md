KATSEYE have pulled out of Daisy Chain Fields, the California festival curated by Olivia Rodrigo, ahead of their scheduled Saturday set.

The group confirmed on Weverse that member Megan sprained her ankle during tour rehearsal and was advised by doctors to rest so she can be ready for their upcoming run of shows. Two other members are already on hiatus.

Organisers have named Devon Again as the replacement, and the rest of the bill is unchanged.
