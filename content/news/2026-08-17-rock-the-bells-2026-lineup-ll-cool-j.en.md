LL Cool J, Lil' Kim, Method Man & Redman and SWV headline this year's [Rock The Bells Festival 2026](/en/concert/en-rock-the-bells-festival-2026-20261129), held November 29th at the Prudential Center in Newark, New Jersey.

The bill also carries a D-Nice & Friends set with LeToya Luckett, David Banner and The LOX. More names follow.

An Amex presale starts August 18th and an artist presale August 19th, with general tickets August 21st at 10am ET. See the [event page](/en/concert/en-rock-the-bells-festival-2026-20261129).
