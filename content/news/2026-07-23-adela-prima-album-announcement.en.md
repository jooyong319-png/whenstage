ADÉLA has announced her new album PRIMA, set for release September 4 via Capitol Records. The record was executive-produced by 100 gecs' Dylan Brady alongside Blake Slatkin.

PRIMA will collect previously released singles "KGB" and "Red Bottoms." With the album art and tracklist details now out, fans can pre-save ahead of the September 4 drop.
