Providence hardcore outfit Peace Test have set *Repercussion*, their first full-length since 2022's *Pry*, for August 14th on Delayed Gratification.

Trevor Vaughan of the Rival Mob and Wound Man produced the 12-track record. Lead single "Total Power" runs barely two minutes at the band's usual extreme speed.

No tour dates have been attached to the release so far.
