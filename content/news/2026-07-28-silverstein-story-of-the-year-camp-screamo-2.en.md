Silverstein and Story of the Year have announced Camp Screamo 2, a 24-date North American co-headlining run following their earlier leg this year. It opens November 16 at Showbox SoDo in Seattle and closes December 13 at Citizens House of Blues in Boston, with Stand Atlantic and Footballhead supporting. Presales began July 28 using the code CAMPSCREAMO through Ticketmaster, and general tickets go up July 31 at 10 a.m. local time.
