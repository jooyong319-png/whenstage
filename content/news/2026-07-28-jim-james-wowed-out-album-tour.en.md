Jim James releases *Wowed Out*, his new solo album, on August 28, and shared the title track alongside the announcement. A solo acoustic tour follows, opening November 28 at the Kentucky Performing Arts Brown Theatre in Louisville and running through April 25 at Moon Crush in Miramar Beach, Florida, with stops in Chicago, New York, Los Angeles, Boston and Boulder. Artist presale starts July 29 at 10 a.m. local, general on-sale July 31.
