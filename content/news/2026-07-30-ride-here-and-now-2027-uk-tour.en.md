Ride have announced "Here And Now," a six-date UK tour that marks their first live dates in three years and leans on the band's classics.

The run opens in Bournemouth before a Brixton Academy show on February 12th, returning to the venue they filmed a live album at in 1992. Birmingham, Leeds, Manchester and Glasgow follow through February 19th.

A presale is running now, with general sale Friday, July 31st at 10 a.m. UK time.
