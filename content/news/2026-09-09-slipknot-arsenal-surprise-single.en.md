Slipknot broke a three-year silence on September 9th with "Arsenal," dropped without warning alongside a video directed by M. Shawn "Clown" Crahan.

It is their first song since Sid Wilson's exit earlier this year and the first cut with drummer Eloy Casagrande, though fans noted how much turntable scratching survives on it.

Jim Root said in July that sessions with producer Matt Wallace had yielded plenty of material. No album date is set.
