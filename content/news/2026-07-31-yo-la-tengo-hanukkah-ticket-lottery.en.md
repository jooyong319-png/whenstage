After scalpers and bots swallowed last week's presale for Yo La Tengo's annual Hanukkah benefit residency, the band has cancelled those orders and will redistribute the seats by lottery.

Requests run via Ticketmaster Request from Friday, July 31st at noon ET to Sunday, August 2nd at 11:59 p.m. ET, two tickets per show. Winners are charged and notified by August 4th.

The shows run December 4th–11th at New York's Bowery Ballroom.
