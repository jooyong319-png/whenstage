The Darkness are marking compilation Crock Of Hits, out November 27th, with a one-off show at East London's Moth Club on September 17th. Capacity is 300 — their next London date is The O2, which holds 20,000.

Dice presale opens at 10am UK time on Tuesday, September 8th, with general sale 48 hours later. An earlier batch sold in error has been cancelled and refunded.

A UK and Ireland arena tour follows in December, with Brothers Osborne supporting.
