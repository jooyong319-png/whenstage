Bruce Springsteen will top the bill at the 20th anniversary Stand Up For Heroes benefit, held November 9th at David Geffen Hall in Lincoln Center, Manhattan.

Alex Warren and The Resilient, a band made up largely of severely wounded US combat veterans, also perform, with comedy sets from Jon Stewart, Hasan Minhaj and Josh Johnson.

The fundraiser runs alongside the New York Comedy Festival and has raised $117 million since 2006. Tickets are on sale now through Lincoln Center.
