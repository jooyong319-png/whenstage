Rachel Chinouriri has announced her second album, I think I spoke too soon, out October 16th on Atlas Artists/Parlophone/Warner Records. Pre-orders are open now.

The follow-up to 2024's What A Devastating Turn Of Events is led by "One song away from crying," a glassy synth-pop track paired with a Dora Paphides-directed video.

The British singer describes the record as an account of her life between ages 22 and 27, covering sobriety and identity.
