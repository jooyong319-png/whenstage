Jorja Smith will play just five North American dates this November, and the general on-sale opens Friday, August 7th at 10 a.m. PT through Ticketmaster and AXS. The artist presale ran Thursday.

The run hits Los Angeles' Orpheum Theatre on the 14th, then San Francisco, Toronto, Brooklyn and Chicago, supporting third album What Are The Odds.

With one date per market, expect the theatre-sized rooms to move fast. A dollar from every ticket goes to PLUS1.
