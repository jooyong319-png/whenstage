Godsmack have bolted a fresh October run onto their ongoing "Rise of Rock" tour, with Dorothy opening every date. The leg starts October 9th in Airway Heights, Washington and closes October 24th at Mohegan Sun Arena in Uncasville, Connecticut, hitting Lincoln, Minneapolis, Green Bay, Milwaukee, Cincinnati and Atlantic City along the way.

A Ticketmaster presale is already running with the code LEGENDS. General on-sale opens Friday, July 31st at 10 a.m. local time.
