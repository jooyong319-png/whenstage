**[Cancelled]** Yours Always, a two-day festival set for October 2nd and 3rd at Utah State Fairpark, has been scrapped less than five weeks after going on sale.

Benson Boone was to headline Friday and Sombr Saturday, with Stephen Sanchez, Labrinth, Joshua Bassett, Daniel Seavey and BORNS also billed. Organizers pulled the website and socials without a statement.

Ticket holders received emails promising refunds and citing "unforeseen circumstances." Buyers should confirm the money has landed on their original payment method.
