Kylie Cantrall will headline her own tour for the first time this fall, backing Valley Girl Problems, her Republic Records debut EP released July 24th.

The 15-date run opens September 25th at The Fillmore Minneapolis and ends October 22nd at The Novo in Los Angeles, by way of Toronto, New York's Terminal 5 and Austin's ACL Live.

An artist presale opens August 5th through her website; general sale is Saturday, August 7th.
