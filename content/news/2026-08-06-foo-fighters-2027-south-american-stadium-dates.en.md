The [Foo Fighters: Take Cover Tour](/en/concert/en-foo-fighters-take-cover-detroit-20260806) is heading south. Four stadium shows open February 23rd at Estadio Centenario in Montevideo, followed by Buenos Aires' Estadio River Plate, Santiago's Parque Estadio Nacional and Lima's Estadio San Marcos.

Each night pairs the band with two regional openers, among them Niña Lobo, Usted Señalemelo and Adelaida. A Rock in Rio headline slot lands September 4th.

Tickets go on sale Thursday, August 13th at 10 a.m. local time via foofighters.com. See full details on the [tour page](/en/concert/en-foo-fighters-take-cover-detroit-20260806).
