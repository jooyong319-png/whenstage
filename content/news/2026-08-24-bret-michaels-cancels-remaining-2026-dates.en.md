Bret Michaels has called off every remaining date on his 2026 "Live And Amplified" tour. The Poison frontman played August 21st in Sugar Hill, Georgia, his first show since surgery for a kidney stone, but doctors and paramedics pulled him from the road straight afterwards.

A statement said he needs several months of rest before returning at full health. The scrapped stops included Texas, Ohio, Vancouver and Minnesota.
