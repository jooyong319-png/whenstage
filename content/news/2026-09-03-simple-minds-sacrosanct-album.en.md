Jim Kerr and Charlie Burchill are approaching a fiftieth year together, and they are marking it with a new record. Sacrosanct, an eight-track set, arrives January 29th on BMG and is open for pre-order now.

Lead single "Beginner Boys" came out September 3rd. Kerr says the song grew out of watching his grandsons grow up and the mixed signals culture sends them about strength and gentleness.

The pair also publish a joint memoir, Our Secrets Are The Same, next week.
