Harry Styles says the 2027 leg closes out "Together Together" for good. It opens with two nights in Phoenix on April 2nd and ends with three at Wembley in late August, with Kylie Minogue, LCD Soundsystem and Tears For Fears among the rotating openers.

There is no dynamic or platinum pricing this time. GA is artist-set and all-in, roughly $60 to $400, plus limited $20 Unpredictable Fun Tickets.

Presale registration closes September 13th; general sales run September 14th to 17th.
