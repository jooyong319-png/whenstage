[Riot Fest 2026](/en/concert/en-riot-fest-2026-20260918) has published its day-by-day stage times, and the Saturday billing is the talking point: Morrissey plays directly before Tool, with Chat Pile and Bad Religion running opposite him.

Friday pits Iggy Pop against GWAR in the same window. On Sunday, 95-year-old William Shatner takes the Rebel Stage between This Is Lorelei and Twin Peaks, ahead of Patti Smith and ICP.

Tickets remain on sale via the festival. Plan your clashes on the [event page](/en/concert/en-riot-fest-2026-20260918).
