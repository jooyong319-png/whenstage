Zach Condon is taking Beirut back to North America for the first time since 2019, with six shows in April 2027 behind *A Study of Losses*.

Two nights at Brooklyn Steel on April 2nd and 3rd open the run, followed by Boston, Washington DC and Chicago, wrapping at Toronto's History on April 13th.

An artist presale ran September 10th; general tickets land September 11th at 10am local time via Ticketmaster and the band's own site.
