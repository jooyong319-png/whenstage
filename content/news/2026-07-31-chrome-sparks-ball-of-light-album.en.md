Jeremy Malvin has confirmed *Ball Of Light*, his first Chrome Sparks album in seven years, for September 11th on Chrome Sounds/AWAL. Pre-orders are open now.

Opening track and lead single "Sleeper" is an unlikely pairing: Khalid's R&B vocal set against Sigur Ros frontman Jonsi's falsetto over airy electronic post-rock.

Malvin, who recently produced for Slayyyter and Lily Allen, calls the 12-track record "me finding myself again." No live dates have been announced yet.
