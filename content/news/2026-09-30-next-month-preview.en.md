September was a month of tour openers. October is a month of tours already in motion, which changes what is worth planning around: fewer one-off nights, more runs where the stop nearest you is one of several.

The month opens the way it closes, with stadiums. [The R&B Tour: Usher & Chris Brown](/en/concert/en-usher-chris-brown-rb-tour-east-rutherford-20261015) plays MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford on the 15th and then Lucas Oil Stadium in [Indianapolis on Halloween night](/en/concert/en-usher-chris-brown-rb-tour-indianapolis-20261031) — the last date on our October calendar, and the only stadium show of the month that comes with a costume excuse. In between, [JAY-Z's 30th Anniversary](/en/concert/en-jay-z-30th-anniversary-los-angeles-20261023) show lands at SoFi Stadium on the 23rd, and [Shakira closes her Madrid residency](/en/concert/en-shakira-las-mujeres-ya-no-lloran-madrid-20261011) at Iberdrola Music on the 11th.

Two runs give you options rather than a single date. The [Jonas Brothers](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-burning-up-all-over-again-philadelphia-20261002) hit Philadelphia on the 2nd, [Indianapolis on the 15th](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-burning-up-all-over-again-indianapolis-20261015) and [Rosemont on the 22nd](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-burning-up-all-over-again-rosemont-20261022). [Phish](/en/concert/en-phish-fall-tour-atlantic-city-20261002) open their October tour at Boardwalk Hall in Atlantic City on the 2nd before [Richmond on the 6th](/en/concert/en-phish-fall-tour-richmond-20261006) — with this band, two stops is two different shows.

The first nights are mostly at the other end of the scale. [Sugarland](/en/concert/en-sugarland-ride-or-die-st-augustine-20261001) open The Ride Or Die Tour tomorrow in St. Augustine, and [Orville Peck](/en/concert/en-orville-peck-man-turned-mule-sacramento-20261028) starts the Man Turned Mule Tour at Channel 24 in Sacramento on the 28th.

And the quiet recommendation: [Nice as Fuck](/en/concert/en-nice-as-fuck-10th-anniversary-pasadena-20261016) play Pasadena on the 16th and a Brooklyn roller rink on the [29th](/en/concert/en-nice-as-fuck-10th-anniversary-brooklyn-20261029). [Steve Lacy](/en/concert/en-steve-lacy-radio-city-20261010) takes Radio City on the 10th. [Tame Impala](/en/concert/en-tame-impala-deadbeat-perth-20261024) reach Perth on the 24th, and [Austin City Limits](/en/concert/en-austin-city-limits-festival-20261002) takes Zilker Park from the 2nd.
