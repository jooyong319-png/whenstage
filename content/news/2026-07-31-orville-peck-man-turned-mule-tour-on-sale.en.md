Public tickets for Orville Peck's "Man Turned Mule Tour" go live Friday, July 31st at 10 a.m. local time via Ticketmaster, closing out a week of VIP, artist and Citi presales.

The 19-date leg starts October 28th in Sacramento and wraps November 22nd in Huntington, New York, with Abbie Callahan supporting. Peck is donating $1 per ticket to The Trevor Project.

The run backs [Mule](/en/concert/en-orville-peck-mule-20260918), out September 18th.
