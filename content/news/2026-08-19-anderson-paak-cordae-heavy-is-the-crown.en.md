Anderson .Paak and Cordae are turning a seven-year working relationship into an album. "Heavy Is the Crown" arrives October 23rd via Apeshit, Mass Appeal and Empire, announced with a trailer and cover art riffing on the 2008 comedy Step Brothers.

The two first connected on "RNP" from Cordae's 2019 debut. Cordae's last solo record was 2024's "The Crossroads."
