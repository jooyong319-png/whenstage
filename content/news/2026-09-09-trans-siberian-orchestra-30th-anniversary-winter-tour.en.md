Trans-Siberian Orchestra mark 30 years since *Christmas Eve and Other Stories* with a rebuilt staging of "The Ghosts of Christmas Eve," running from Green Bay on November 19th to Buffalo and St. Louis on December 30th.

The company again splits into Eastern and Western troupes, each with its own cast and pyrotechnics, so most cities get two shows.

Fan club sales opened September 10th, a Live Nation presale follows on the 17th, and general tickets go on sale September 18th.
