Glenn Hughes has ended his touring career. The 75-year-old singer-bassist said scans and an echocardiogram showed he needs another open-heart surgery, leaving him "no option and no choice."

Hughes had already scrapped a spring US tour. His final performance was May 30th with the Dead Daisies, billed as the Purple Daisies; a second show under that name set for October 2nd is now cancelled.

Nuno Bettencourt, Matt Sorum and Joe Satriani were among those posting support.
