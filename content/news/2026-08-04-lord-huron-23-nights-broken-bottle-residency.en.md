Lord Huron are taking a fictional desert honky-tonk on the road. "23 Nights at the Broken Bottle" turns each stage into the saloon from the band's mythology, billed as An Evening With Lord Huron, with no opener and deep cuts alongside the hits.

The run starts December 1st at Nashville's Ryman and ends January 11th at the Wiltern, with two-night stands in Chicago, Brooklyn, Vancouver and Seattle.

An artist presale opens August 5th; general tickets follow August 7th.
