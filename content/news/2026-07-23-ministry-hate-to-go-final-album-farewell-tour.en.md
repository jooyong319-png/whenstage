Industrial pioneers Ministry have announced their final album, Hate To Go (Take Out Or Delivery), due October 30, and shared lead single and video "Burned Out."

The band confirmed a handful of 2026 U.S. dates, including Red Rocks on September 15, Louder Than Life, Chicago's Salt Shed on October 3, and Sick New World Texas. A Goodbye Europe Tour follows in April 2027, with more U.S. dates to come.
