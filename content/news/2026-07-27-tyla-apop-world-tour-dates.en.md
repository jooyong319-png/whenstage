Tyla has announced [The A*POP World Tour](/en/concert/en-tyla-apop-world-tour-london-20261015), a 34-date run behind her sophomore album *APOP*, released July 24. It opens October 12 in Paris and passes through Amsterdam, London and Berlin before the North American leg starts November 12 in Wheatland, California, with stops in Chicago, Brooklyn and Los Angeles. Lagos, Cape Town and Johannesburg close the itinerary. An artist presale runs July 29 at 10 a.m. local via Laylo, with general on-sale July 31 through Ticketmaster.
