Megadeth have detailed "Breakout: Hibernation of the Nations," the UK and European chapter of their long farewell. Black Label Society and Testament support throughout.

The 24-date leg opens March 9th at The O2 Belfast and runs through London's OVO Arena Wembley, Manchester, Glasgow, Paris, Amsterdam, Berlin, Krakow and Milan before finishing April 13th in Lisbon.

Cyber Army fan club presale starts Tuesday, August 4th, an artist presale August 5th, and general tickets Friday, August 7th via Ticketmaster.
