November was about Los Angeles. December splits two ways: a British band finally playing the rooms its hits always deserved, and a pre-Christmas weekend where New York runs out of free nights.

Start in the UK. The Darkness open their Best Of The Darkness tour at the [OVO Hydro in Glasgow on December 8th](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-glasgow-20261208), the first night of their largest headline run in twenty years. It moves fast: [Leeds](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-leeds-20261209) on the 9th, [Manchester](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-manchester-20261211) on the 11th, [Birmingham](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-birmingham-20261212) on the 12th, [Cardiff](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-cardiff-20261213) on the 13th and [Brighton](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-brighton-20261215) on the 15th, before the finale at [The O2 in London](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-london-20261216) on the 16th, the band's first headline show there. Brothers Osborne and British rock band A support every night. Earlier in the month, [EsDeeKid plays OVO Arena Wembley](/en/concert/en-esdeekid-council-house-rat-london-20261203) on the 3rd, the biggest headline date of his career so far.

Now New York. From December 18th to 21st the area stacks five big nights into four days. [Lord Huron take Kings Theatre in Brooklyn](/en/concert/en-lord-huron-broken-bottle-brooklyn-20261218) on the 18th and 19th, the only NYC stop of their Broken Bottle residency tour. On the 19th, [Amorphis and Insomnium close their co-headline tour at Irving Plaza](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-new-york-20261219) while the [Jonas Brothers play UBS Arena in Belmont Park](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-burning-up-all-over-again-belmont-park-20261219). Two nights later the brothers wrap The Burning Up Tour All Over Again at [Prudential Center in Newark](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-prudential-center-20261221). If you're choosing one Saturday ticket, that 19th is the hard call.

The holiday shows also cluster that weekend. [Trans-Siberian Orchestra play Pittsburgh twice on the 19th](/en/concert/en-trans-siberian-orchestra-pittsburgh-20261219), at 2:30 p.m. and 7:30 p.m., then [Philadelphia twice on the 20th](/en/concert/en-trans-siberian-orchestra-philadelphia-20261220), at 3 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. The matinees remain the family-friendly option.

Two residencies bookend the month. [Yo La Tengo return to the Bowery Ballroom](/en/concert/en-yo-la-tengo-hanukkah-new-york-20261204) for eight Hanukkah nights from the 4th to the 11th, with no song repeated across the run. And on New Year's Eve, the [Backstreet Boys see in 2027 at Sphere](/en/concert/en-backstreet-boys-into-the-millennium-nye-las-vegas-20261231), with two more nights on January 1st and 2nd.

Seoul has its own visiting-act week: [Khalid at KINTEX](/en/concert/en-khalid-always-summer-somewhere-seoul-20261205) on the 5th at 7 p.m., his first Korean headline show in eight years, and [Slowdive at KBS Arena](/en/concert/en-slowdive-live-2026-seoul-20261208) on the 8th at 8 p.m.
