Fontaines D.C. will headline Slane Castle in County Meath on June 26th, 2027, with Hayley Williams, Damien Dempsey and Madra Salach also on the bill.

That makes them only the third Irish act to top the 80,000-capacity amphitheatre, after Thin Lizzy in 1981 and U2 in 2001.

The band teased "Slane 2027" on screen at Electric Picnic. Presale runs from 10am BST on September 1st, with general sale at 10am BST on Thursday, September 3rd.
