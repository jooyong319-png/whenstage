JAY-Z has added stadium dates to his 30th anniversary run, following three hometown shows at Yankee Stadium earlier this month. He plays London's Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on September 4 and 5, Stade de France in Paris on September 10, and [Los Angeles' SoFi Stadium on October 23 and 24](/en/concert/en-jay-z-30th-anniversary-los-angeles-20261023). Presales begin July 29 at 10 a.m. local, with O2 and Virgin Media access in London and Citi access in Los Angeles. General on-sale follows July 30 via Ticketmaster.
