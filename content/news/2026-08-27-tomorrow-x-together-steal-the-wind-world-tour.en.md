TOMORROW X TOGETHER have laid out 32 dates for their "STEAL THE WIND" world tour, opening November 13th with three nights in Seoul before stops across Asia.

The North American leg begins May 4th in Tacoma and runs through Inglewood, Mexico City, Atlanta and Chicago. Europe follows in June, taking in Manchester, Milan, Paris, Amsterdam and Cologne before two closing nights in Hong Kong.

Venues and on-sale dates are still to come, and BIGHIT MUSIC says more stops are coming.
