After three sold-out Madison Square Garden nights, Kevin, Joe and Nick Jonas are taking "The Burning Up Tour All Over Again" nationwide. The 45-city Live Nation run opens September 25th at Boston's TD Garden and closes December 21st at Newark's Prudential Center.

Magnus Ferrell, Franklin Jonas and All Time Low support on select dates, with stops in Toronto, Miami, Los Angeles and Seattle.

A Spotify Reserved presale opens August 18th at 8:28am local; general tickets follow August 21st at 10am.
