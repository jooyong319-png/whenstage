Charli xcx is releasing discounted "Angel Tickets" for the Music, Fashion, Film Tour. The request window opened August 10th at noon PT and closes Wednesday, August 12th at 11:59 p.m. PT.

Priced at $20 plus a $5 service charge, they come in non-transferable pairs. Seats are random, from limited view to GA floor, and revealed at will call on show night.
