Wynonna Judd has mapped out "The Hard Truth Tour," backing her album of the same name, due October 2nd on ANTI- Records.

The 15-date run opens October 8th at New York's Bowery Ballroom and closes November 21st in Gary, Indiana, hitting Houston, Tulsa, St. Louis and Tampa. Troubadour Blue opens every night.

Wynation fan club presale starts Tuesday, August 4th, a Live Nation presale August 5th, and general tickets Friday, August 7th.
