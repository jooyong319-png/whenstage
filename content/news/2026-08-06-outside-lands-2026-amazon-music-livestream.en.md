The San Francisco festival is sold out, but Amazon Music will carry select Golden Gate Park sets from Friday, August 7th through Sunday, August 9th. Coverage starts at 3 p.m. PDT daily via the Amazon Music app, Prime Video, Twitch and Fire TV.

For the first time there are two channels at once. Friday brings Charli xcx, Turnstile, Clipse and Wet Leg; Saturday closes with The xx and The Strokes; Sunday features Baby Keem and two Death Cab for Cutie sets.
