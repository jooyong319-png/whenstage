Cabins for the 15th 70,000 Tons of Metal went on public sale on Monday, September 7th at midday ET, in single and group configurations via the festival's own site.

The 3,000-capacity cruise sails from Miami aboard Royal Caribbean's Freedom of the Seas on January 14th, stopping in Puerto Plata before returning January 18th. Half the 60-band bill is confirmed, including Arch Enemy, Exodus, HammerFall, Moonspell and Uli Jon Roth.

Wolfheart will also premiere their unannounced next album on board.
