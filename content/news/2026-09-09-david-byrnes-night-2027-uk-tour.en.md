David Byrne's Night — the Talking Heads and Robert Burns tribute founded by Dundee's Lola Stephen — has confirmed a five-date UK run for January 2027.

It starts at Manchester's New Century Hall on the 15th, then visits Leeds, Brighton and Glasgow, with a January 23rd show at London's Troxy that will be the project's largest headline date so far.

Members of English Teacher, The Pogues, The Horrors and Goat Girl are confirmed for London. Tickets are on sale now.
