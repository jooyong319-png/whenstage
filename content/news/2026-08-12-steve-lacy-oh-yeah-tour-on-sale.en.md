Steve Lacy has plotted his first headline tour since 2022 behind third album *Oh yeah?*, released July 17th with guests including SZA, Erykah Badu and Tyler, the Creator.

The 27-city North American run launches September 27th at Minneapolis' The Armory and wraps November 30th in Vancouver. Marquee stops include Radio City Music Hall, Red Rocks Amphitheatre and the Hollywood Bowl.

General tickets go on sale Friday, August 14th at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster.
