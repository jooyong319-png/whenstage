Gregg Alexander and Danielle Brisebois have quietly put out "One Night Only (Break Loose Break Free!)," the first newly recorded track credited to New Radicals since the band's 1998 debut album.

Co-written and performed with Nick Lashley, the piano-driven cut serves as the theme for the Monica Barbaro and Callum Turner romcom of the same name.

King Princess and Este Haim also contributed to the soundtrack, which is streaming now.
