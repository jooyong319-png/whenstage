Lil Uzi Vert's slot at [Lollapalooza 2026](/en/concert/en-lollapalooza-2026-20260730) was pulled on Friday, with the festival citing weather and an onstage announcement pointing to travel problems.

He turned up in Grant Park regardless, crashing Major Lazer's set for "Just Wanna Rock." The rapper also marked his 31st birthday by surprise-releasing the EP Maverick "Almost Forever," his first project since 2024's Eternal Atake 2.

The EP is streaming now. See full details on the [event page](/en/concert/en-lollapalooza-2026-20260730).
