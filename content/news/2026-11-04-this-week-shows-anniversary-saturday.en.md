Most weeks, the busy night is busy because a lot of tours happen to pass through. This Saturday is busy for a stranger reason: three bands are playing whole albums from the mid-2000s, front to back, on the same date, on two different continents.

In Incheon, [My Chemical Romance bring The Black Parade 2026 to Paradise City](/en/concert/en-mcr-black-parade-2026-incheon-20261107) on November 7th, performing the record in full for its twentieth anniversary. It is the band's first Korean concert in about 18 years. The show was first set for April 18th and moved to November, with tickets from the original date carried over rather than reissued. If you bought in spring, your ticket still stands.

Hours later in Los Angeles, [Jimmy Eat World and The Format share a stage for the first time at the Hollywood Bowl](/en/concert/en-jimmy-eat-world-the-format-hollywood-bowl-20261107). Jimmy Eat World play Bleed American for its 25th, The Middle and Hear You Me included, and The Format play Dog Problems for its 20th, one of only two full-album shows they have scheduled this year.

Saturday doesn't stop there. [Olivia Rodrigo opens two nights of The Unraveled Tour in Philadelphia](/en/concert/en-olivia-rodrigo-unraveled-tour-philadelphia-20261107) at Xfinity Mobile Arena, doors at 6 p.m. and the show at 7 p.m. on both the 7th and 8th. In Seoul, [the 2026 Korea Grand Music Awards](/en/concert/en-kgma-2026-20261107) take over Gocheok Sky Dome for Artist Day, with ILLIT, TREASURE and P1Harmony among the acts, before ATEEZ, ITZY and RIIZE headline Music Day on Sunday; the livestream on TikTok Live is the practical way in from abroad. Across town, [GOT7's Park Jinyoung opens his first solo tour, CODE#,](/en/concert/en-park-jinyoung-code-seoul-20261107) at Jangchung Arena at 6 p.m. KST.

The weekdays carry the long runs. [Usher and Chris Brown's R&B Tour starts five nights at Mercedes-Benz Stadium](/en/concert/en-usher-chris-brown-rb-tour-atlanta-20261105) in Atlanta on Thursday the 5th, the same day [Pentatonix open their Christmas leg at the Tacoma Dome](/en/concert/en-pentatonix-christmas-in-the-city-tacoma-20261105). [Max Changmin's three sold-out Resonance nights](/en/concert/en-max-changmin-resonance-seoul-20261106) begin Friday in Seoul, his first solo concerts in Korea. [Quadeca plays The Showbox in Seattle](/en/concert/en-quadeca-life-1-tour-seattle-20261106) that night, four days before his tour ends in Los Angeles.

Monday the 9th brings [Bruce Springsteen to Stand Up for Heroes](/en/concert/en-stand-up-for-heroes-2026-new-york-20261109), the veterans benefit's twentieth year at David Geffen Hall, with Jon Stewart and Hasan Minhaj on the comedy side. And Wednesday the 11th splits two ways: [Allison Russell at The Showbox](/en/concert/en-allison-russell-hour-of-chaos-seattle-20261111), or [Steve Lacy at Red Rocks](/en/concert/en-steve-lacy-oh-yeah-morrison-20261111), the first of his three big November rooms.

If you only make one show, make it an anniversary one. Those setlists won't come around again.
