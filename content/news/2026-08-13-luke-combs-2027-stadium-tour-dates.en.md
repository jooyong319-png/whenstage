Luke Combs has added a 2027 North American stadium leg to his "My Kinda Saturday Night Tour," opening April 3rd at AT&T Stadium in Arlington and closing June 26th at San Diego's Petco Park.

The 12-date run stops in Minneapolis, Detroit, Philadelphia, Edmonton and Vancouver, staged in the round throughout. Treaty Oak Revival, Avery Anna, Shenandoah and Wyatt McCubbin open every night.

Bootleggers presales start August 19th; general tickets follow August 21st. See full details on the [Arlington opening night page](/en/concert/en-luke-combs-my-kinda-saturday-night-arlington-20270403).
