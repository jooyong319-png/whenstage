Ozzfest has named Villa Park in Birmingham, England as the home of its 2027 revival. The ground hosted 2025's "Back to the Beginning" concert, the final performances by Black Sabbath and Ozzy Osbourne.

The Instagram announcement carries no specific date yet.

Sharon and Jack Osbourne said earlier this year that the festival would restart in England before travelling to America; no US venue is confirmed. The last Ozzfest was a 2018 one-off in Los Angeles.
