The German rock veterans have pushed the rest of their 2026 calendar into next year, including an eight-night Planet Hollywood residency and three Mexican arena shows.

The Vegas run now spans September 9th through 25th, 2027, while Guadalajara, Mexico City and Monterrey land on August 27th, August 30th and September 2nd. Live Nation announced the Vegas move; promoter Ocesa handled the Mexico dates.

Ticket holders should check their point of purchase for exchange terms.
