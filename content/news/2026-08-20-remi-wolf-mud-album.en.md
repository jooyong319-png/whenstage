Remi Wolf has dated her new album "Mud" for October 9th via Island. The California singer-producer shared cover art and a beach-set video for opening track "Bottle."

Eleven songs make the cut, including July's comeback single "Twiggy," which she debuted during a surprise turn at Hayley Williams' Newport Folk Festival set.

It follows 2024's "Big Ideas." Pre-orders are open now.
