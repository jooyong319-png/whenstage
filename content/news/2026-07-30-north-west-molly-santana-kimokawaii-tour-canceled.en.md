**[Cancelled]** Ticket holders for the "Molly x North Kimokawaii Tour" should check their orders. Almost every date on the 14-show co-headline run now reads cancelled on ticketing sites or has vanished from venue calendars.

The August 5th Dallas opener and an August 17th Toronto date are flagged on Ticketmaster; AXS lists the August 27th Los Angeles show the same way. A promotional site for the tour is offline, and neither artist has commented.
