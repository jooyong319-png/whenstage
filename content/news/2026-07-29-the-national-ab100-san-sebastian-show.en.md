The National will play their first concert since 2024 with a one-off show at the AB100 festival in San Sebastian, Spain.

Billed as "The National And Friends," the October 18th event at Kursaal Donostia pairs the band with Kevin Morby, Ben Howard, Jon Hopkins, Mina Tindle, Anais Mitchell, Ragnar Kjartansson and Arcade Fire's Richard Reed Parry, among others.

Tickets go on sale Monday, August 3rd at 11:30 a.m. Central European Time.
