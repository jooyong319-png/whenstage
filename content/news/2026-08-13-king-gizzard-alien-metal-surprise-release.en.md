King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard confirmed on August 13th that *Alien Metal*, their 28th album, arrives the very next day.

The rave-themed record was built on a modular synth setup the band nicknamed "Nathan," the same rig powering their recent Rave Shows. Singles "Level 5," "Alien Metal" and "Kill for the Steel" previewed the darker electronic turn.

The group headline their own Field of Vision II festival in Buena Vista, Colorado this weekend, then play four New York shows.
