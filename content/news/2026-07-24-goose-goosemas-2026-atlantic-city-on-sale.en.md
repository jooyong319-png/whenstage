Jam band Goose will hold its 13th annual Goosemas celebration December 11-12 at Atlantic City's historic Jim Whelan Boardwalk Hall, the group's first Goosemas run in the city.

Following VIP packages and presales earlier in the week, remaining tickets go on general sale Friday, July 24 at 10 a.m. ET via Ticketmaster, with single-night tickets and two-day passes available.
