Morgan Wallen has officially released "Been By Now," out July 24 via Big Loud, Republic and Mercury Records. He first teased the track online in June and gave it a live debut during a sold-out Baltimore stop on his Still The Problem Tour.

Produced by longtime collaborator Joey Moi, the song arrives as the tour rolls on through the summer. Fans can stream it now and catch it live on upcoming dates.
