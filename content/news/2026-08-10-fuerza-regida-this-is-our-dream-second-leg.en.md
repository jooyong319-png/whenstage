Fuerza Regida have mapped a second leg of their "This Is Our Dream Tour," ten amphitheater and arena dates opening October 3rd in Tinley Park, Illinois and closing November 2nd at Red Rocks.

Presales ran August 11th for Verizon, Spotify and Citi holders, with an artist window August 12th. The public on-sale is Friday, August 14th at 10 a.m. local.
