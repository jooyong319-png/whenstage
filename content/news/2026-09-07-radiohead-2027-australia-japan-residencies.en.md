Radiohead's 2027 touring year is 21 arena shows, built as residencies. Perth's RAC Arena hosts four nights from May 3rd, then six each at Melbourne's Rod Laver Arena and Sydney's Afterpay Arena, and five at GMO Arena Saitama in June.

There is no open on-sale: to blunt bots and resellers, tickets go only to fans registered on the band's official site, capped at four each.

It matches Ed O'Brien's plan of 20 shows a year, one continent at a time.
