Jade Lilitri's Oso Oso have booked a fall club tour across the US and Toronto. The "An Exchange of Information Tour" opens October 7th at Brighton Music Hall in Allston and wraps November 24th in St. Louis.

Leisure Hour and Bad Luck support in October; Liquid Mike and Bugsy take November.

Tickets go on sale Friday at 10:00 a.m. ET, when Laylo subscribers also get a link to new song "The Nights."
