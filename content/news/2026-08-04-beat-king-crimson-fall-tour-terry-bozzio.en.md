BEAT, the supergroup built around King Crimson's catalog, return this fall with a lineup change: Terry Bozzio takes over from Danny Carey, who is tied up with Tool. Adrian Belew, Tony Levin and Steve Vai continue.

The 40-date North American run opens October 9th in Santa Cruz and closes December 3rd in Riverside, California, expanding beyond the '80s material into Crimson's '90s work.

A presale is live with code BEATTOUR; general tickets open August 7th.
