Wolves In The Throne Room will release "Estuary," their tenth album, on October 23rd through Relapse. Pre-orders are live.

Frontman Nathan Weaver describes a six-track concept record set a thousand years after civilization collapses, with survivors crossing haunted ruins where estuaries hold physical magic.

The band self-produced at their Owl Lodge studio in Olympia, Washington. Weaver also directed the video for lead single "Ghosts In The Obelisks."
