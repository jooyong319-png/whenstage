Most weeks our calendar is about where to be standing on a given night. This week the busiest date isn't a show at all. Friday, October 16th carries five full-length records, tied with the 23rd for the busiest release day on our October calendar, and they come from five different countries.

The headline pair are both long-awaited returns. [Fontaines D.C.'s Dopamine Chamber](/en/concert/en-fontaines-dc-dopamine-chamber-20261016) is the Dublin band's fifth album, back on XL Recordings with James Ford producing again after *Romance*, and led by the single "Marianne". [Angèle's Instinct](/en/concert/en-angele-instinct-20261016) is her first full-length since *Nonante-Cinq* in 2021: thirteen tracks with Justice, Soulwax, Caroline Polachek and SebastiAn among the contributors.

[The Avalanches' No Bad Memories](/en/concert/en-the-avalanches-no-bad-memories-20261016) ends a gap of its own. It is the Melbourne duo's fourth album and first since 2020, with earlier tracks drawing on Karen O and Jamie xx. [Rachel Chinouriri's I think I spoke too soon](/en/concert/en-rachel-chinouriri-i-think-i-spoke-too-soon-20261016) is her second record, following her 2024 debut, with Lucy Dacus among the new collaborators.

The one to watch if you like your albums followed quickly by a stage is [Insomnium's Netherworlds](/en/concert/en-insomnium-netherworlds-20261016), the Finnish band's tenth. A month later they co-headline North America with Amorphis, opening at [Paradise Rock Club in Boston on November 17th](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-boston-20261117), passing [The Showbox in Seattle on December 2nd](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-seattle-20261202) and closing at [Irving Plaza in New York on December 19th](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-new-york-20261219). That is about four weeks to learn the new songs before the first night.

Fontaines D.C. fans have a longer runway. The band's biggest shows yet are [Slane Castle on June 26th, 2027](/en/concert/en-fontaines-dc-slane-castle-20270626), plus a [second night on the 27th](/en/concert/en-fontaines-dc-slane-castle-20270627) added after demand for the first. General sale opened in September, so Friday is a chance to hear the record those nights will lean on.

One more thread runs through the week, and only shows up when you put the dates side by side: SHINee. [TAEMIN plays Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles](/en/concert/en-taemin-liminal-crypto-com-arena-20261015) on Thursday the 15th, part of his first arena-scale LiMiNaL run. Five days later, at 6pm KST on Tuesday the 20th, bandmate [ONEW releases his first single album, Chakgak](/en/concert/en-onew-chakgak-20261020), whose third title track arrives with the album after two earlier ones in late September and early October.
