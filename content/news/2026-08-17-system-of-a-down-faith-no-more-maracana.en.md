System of a Down and Faith No More will play a single "One Night Only" concert at Rio de Janeiro's Maracana Stadium on January 15th, 2027, extending their [Australia and New Zealand 2027 run](/en/concert/en-system-of-a-down-faith-no-more-sydney-20270122).

The date is currently set to be Faith No More's first live show since 2016.

Itau bank presales begin August 19th, with general tickets following August 21st through Eventim. The joint run then continues through Australia and New Zealand until February 7th.
