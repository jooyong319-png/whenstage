Anyone outside Grant Park can still watch [Lollapalooza 2026](/en/concert/en-lollapalooza-2026-20260730), with dozens of sets carried on Hulu and Disney+ across all four days.

Coverage opened Thursday, July 30th and runs through Sunday, August 2nd, starting at 2:45 p.m. CT daily. Charli xcx, Lorde, Tate McRae, The xx, Turnstile, Ethel Cain, JENNIE and aespa are among those confirmed. Set times are still to come.

See full details on the [event page](/en/concert/en-lollapalooza-2026-20260730).
