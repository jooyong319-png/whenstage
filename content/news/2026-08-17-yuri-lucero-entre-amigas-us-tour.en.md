Mexican pop institutions Yuri and Lucero are joining forces for the co-headlining Entre Amigas Tour across the United States.

The run opens November 27th at Sugar Land's Smart Financial Centre, with November stops in San Antonio and Hidalgo, then resumes April 15th, 2027 in San Jose.

The 2027 leg visits Los Angeles, San Diego, Glendale, El Paso, Chicago and Fairfax before closing May 9th at Newark's Prudential Center. Yuri is still touring behind her April album "Senora."
