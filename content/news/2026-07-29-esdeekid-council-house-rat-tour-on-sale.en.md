EsDeeKid has mapped out a 48-date "Council House Rat Tour" spanning North America, Europe and the UK. It opens September 12th in Charlotte and works through Brooklyn, Toronto, Chicago, Los Angeles and Austin before the overseas leg begins November 2nd in Helsinki.

The run peaks with London's OVO Arena Wembley on December 3rd and two hometown Liverpool nights on December 10th and 11th. Presales began July 30th; general on-sale is July 31st at 10 a.m. local via Ticketmaster.
