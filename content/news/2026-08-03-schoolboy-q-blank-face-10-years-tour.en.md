ScHoolboy Q is taking Blank Face LP back on the road for its tenth anniversary, opening October 1st at Atlanta's Tabernacle and ending October 17th at Brooklyn Bowl in Las Vegas.

The 10-city run also stops at Brooklyn Paramount, DC's Fillmore and Oakland's Fox Theater, with two Los Angeles nights.

Artist, Citi and Live Nation presales all begin Tuesday, August 4th. General tickets follow Friday, August 7th on Ticketmaster.
