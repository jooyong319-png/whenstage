Backstreet Boys have stretched their "Into The Millennium" Sphere residency into 2027, adding December 31st, January 1st and January 2nd. It is the second year running that they close the calendar in Las Vegas.

A fan club presale opens August 18th at 10 a.m. PT, with an artist presale the next day. Sign-ups close August 17th. General tickets follow August 21st.

See full details on the [New Year's Eve show page](/en/concert/en-backstreet-boys-into-the-millennium-nye-las-vegas-20261231).
