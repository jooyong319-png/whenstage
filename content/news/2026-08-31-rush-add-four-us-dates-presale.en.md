RUSH have added four arena shows to their [Fifty Something reunion tour](/en/concert/en-rush-fifty-something-new-york-20260801): Houston's Toyota Center on October 1st, St. Louis' Enterprise Center on October 21st, Cincinnati's Heritage Bank Center on October 23rd and Pittsburgh's PPG Paints Arena on November 15th.

Citi cardmember presales open September 1st at 10am local time, with the general on-sale Friday, September 4th via Ticketmaster.

Geddy Lee and Alex Lifeson said the band is holding up well enough to add cities. See full details on the [event page](/en/concert/en-rush-fifty-something-new-york-20260801).
