William Shatner appears to have revealed one of Coachella's first 2027 bookings: his own. Asked on CNN what he was excited about, the 95-year-old actor talked up his metal band and named "the one in the desert" among its upcoming dates.

The group, The \*uckers, makes its live debut at Riot Fest in Chicago on September 20th, ahead of a first album titled What the F Is Heavy Metal.

Coachella has not yet confirmed its 2027 lineup.
