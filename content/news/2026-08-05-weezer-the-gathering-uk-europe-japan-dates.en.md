Weezer have taken "Weezer: The Gathering" global. New 2027 dates cover five Japanese shows in February and a May–June swing through Ireland, the UK, France, the Netherlands and Scandinavia, with Taking Back Sunday supporting overseas.

The announcement lands alongside "C.E.O.," a third single from the band's self-titled 16th album, due August 21st on Reprise. Its video features Tony Hawk, Michael Peña and Christopher Mintz-Plasse.

The previously announced 32-date North American leg begins September 8th in Sacramento.
