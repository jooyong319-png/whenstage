Last month's story was tours already in motion. November's is how many of them end up in the same city. Outside Seoul, no city on our calendar gets more November nights than Los Angeles, and one artist runs straight through the middle of it.

That artist is Steve Lacy. The Oh yeah? Tour, behind his third solo album, takes him to [Red Rocks Amphitheatre on November 11th](/en/concert/en-steve-lacy-oh-yeah-morrison-20261111), then onto the bill at [Camp Flog Gnaw Carnival](/en/concert/en-camp-flog-gnaw-carnival-2026-20261114) at the Dodger Stadium lot on the 14th and 15th alongside Tyler, the Creator, Turnstile, Future and Don Toliver. A week later he makes his [Hollywood Bowl headline debut on the 21st](/en/concert/en-steve-lacy-hollywood-bowl-20261121), the hometown night of a run that wraps in Vancouver on the 30th. Flog Gnaw passes are already gone, with only a waitlist left, so the Bowl is the surer ticket in LA this month. Syd, also on the Flog Gnaw bill, brings her own [Beard Tour to the Tabernacle in Atlanta](/en/concert/en-syd-beard-tour-atlanta-20261124) on the 24th.

Around him, Los Angeles keeps filling. The Bowl opens the month with [Jimmy Eat World and The Format](/en/concert/en-jimmy-eat-world-the-format-hollywood-bowl-20261107) on the 7th, each playing an anniversary album in full, their first shared stage. [Jorja Smith opens her short North American run at the Orpheum](/en/concert/en-jorja-smith-what-are-the-odds-los-angeles-20261114) on the 14th, the same day as Flog Gnaw. Then Saturday the 21st stacks up: Lacy at the Bowl and [the first of KATSEYE's two hometown nights at Crypto.com Arena](/en/concert/en-katseye-wildworld-tour-los-angeles-20261121), two days after [Oakland](/en/concert/en-katseye-wildworld-tour-oakland-20261119).

The biggest rooms belong to [Usher and Chris Brown's R&B Tour](/en/concert/en-usher-chris-brown-rb-tour-atlanta-20261105), which settles in for five nights at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta from November 5th, then closes its LA run with an added [fourth SoFi Stadium show on the 16th](/en/concert/en-usher-chris-brown-rb-tour-los-angeles-20261116). Book a long night: the show runs close to three hours.

And the holidays arrive early. [Pentatonix open their Christmas in the City leg at the Tacoma Dome](/en/concert/en-pentatonix-christmas-in-the-city-tacoma-20261105) on the 5th, [Lindsey Stirling launches the Snow Waltz Holiday Tour](/en/concert/en-lindsey-stirling-snow-waltz-hollywood-20261118) in Hollywood, Florida on the 18th, and [Trans-Siberian Orchestra play Denver twice on the 21st](/en/concert/en-trans-siberian-orchestra-denver-20261121), at 3 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. If you have family to take to one, those matinees are the easy pick.
