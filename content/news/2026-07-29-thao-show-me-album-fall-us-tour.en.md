THAO has revealed Show Me, out October 30th via Kill Rock Stars and co-produced with Tune-Yards' Merrill Garbus and Nate Brenner. Guests include Neko Case, The Linda Lindas and Black Pumas' Adrian Quesada.

A six-date November run follows: Los Angeles, San Francisco, Kingston, New York, Providence and Brattleboro. One dollar from each ticket goes to Working Families Power.

Lead single "Sick Of The Times" is streaming now.
