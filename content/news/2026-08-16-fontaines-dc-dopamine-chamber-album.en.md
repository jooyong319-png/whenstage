Fontaines D.C. have confirmed that their fifth album, "Dopamine Chamber," is coming via XL Recordings. No release date has been attached yet.

The band unveiled the title on social media with a video set to "Six Shot Morning," one of two new songs debuted in Cadiz. A numbered first pressing was sold through their store for 24 hours at $28.03, shipping in late 2026.

The reveal followed their La Route du Rock headline set, where Kurt Vile joined them for "Roman Holiday."
