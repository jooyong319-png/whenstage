Most weeks in our calendar are middle-of-tour stops. This one is almost all first nights and last nights, which is a different thing entirely — opening-night sets get rewritten by the second week, and finales tend to carry guests.

The openers come fast. [Rebecca Black: Exhibitionism on Tour](/en/concert/en-rebecca-black-exhibitionism-tour-boston-20260923) starts the 27-date run tonight at Royale in Boston. The next night [Lucinda Williams: World's Gone Wrong Tour](/en/concert/en-lucinda-williams-worlds-gone-wrong-atlanta-20260924) opens with the first of two nights at Variety Playhouse in Atlanta. On the 25th, [Kylie Cantrall: The Valley Girl Problems Tour](/en/concert/en-kylie-cantrall-valley-girl-problems-minneapolis-20260925) begins her first-ever headlining run at The Fillmore Minneapolis, a 7pm start, while [Icona Pop: Ritual Fall Tour](/en/concert/en-icona-pop-ritual-tour-brooklyn-20260925) opens a short club run at Baby's All Right. [Michelle Branch: Everywhere and Back Again Tour](/en/concert/en-michelle-branch-everywhere-back-again-seattle-20260927) starts the 27th at The Showbox in Seattle, and [Audrey Hobert: The Staircase To Stardom Tour](/en/concert/en-audrey-hobert-staircase-to-stardom-brooklyn-20260929) opens its final leg at Brooklyn Paramount on the 29th.

Thursday the 24th is the week's pile-up. Besides Atlanta, [Slothrust: The Dystopian Theatre Tour](/en/concert/en-slothrust-dystopian-theatre-tour-boston-20260924) takes over the same Boston room Rebecca Black plays the night before, and [Neil Young & The Chrome Hearts and Willie Nelson & Family](/en/concert/en-neil-young-willie-nelson-philadelphia-20260924) play Philadelphia's Mann Center — the second of only two co-headline dates, and Young's main run of the autumn after his European shows were scrapped.

Both festivals land on the same weekend: [Bourbon & Beyond](/en/concert/en-bourbon-and-beyond-2026-20260924) runs four days in Louisville from the 24th, and Goldenvoice's first [Ocean Way Festival](/en/concert/en-ocean-way-festival-2026-20260926) takes the sand at Santa Monica Beach on the 26th and 27th.

Then the closer: [Erykah Badu & The Alchemist](/en/concert/en-erykah-badu-alchemist-hollywood-bowl-20260929) finish their September tour at the Hollywood Bowl on the 29th, with Smino supporting. If you want something to play on the drive, [Brandi Carlile's Returning To Myself (Sad Gay Version)](/en/concert/en-brandi-carlile-returning-to-myself-sad-gay-version-20260925) arrives on the 25th.
