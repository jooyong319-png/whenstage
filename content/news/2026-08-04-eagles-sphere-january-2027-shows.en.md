The Eagles keep extending the longest residency in Sphere's history. Four more Las Vegas nights, on January 15th, 16th, 22nd and 23rd, push [Eagles at Sphere - 2027 Dates](/en/concert/en-eagles-sphere-residency-las-vegas-20270115) to 72 total shows.

It is a reversal of sorts: Don Henley told CBS in February that 2026 would "probably" be the band's last year.

An artist presale starts August 12th, followed by general tickets August 14th. See full details on the [event page](/en/concert/en-eagles-sphere-residency-las-vegas-20270115).
