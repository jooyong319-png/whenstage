Jake Bugg has set his seventh album, Alamo, for November 13th via Thirty Tigers, led by the country-leaning single "Never Can Tell." He calls it his most exposed writing yet, produced by Jimmy Hogarth and Steve McKewan.

A stripped-back solo run follows in February 2027, opening at Manchester's Albert Hall on the 6th and closing at London's Union Chapel on the 26th, via Dublin, Edinburgh, Glasgow and Bristol.

Presales began September 4th; general sale is 10am BST on Friday, September 11th.
