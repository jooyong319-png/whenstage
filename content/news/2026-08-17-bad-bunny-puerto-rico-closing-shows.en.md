Bad Bunny is ending the "Debi Tirar Mas Fotos World Tour" where it began. The two-night "Cerramos en Casa" finale hits San Juan's Hiram Bithorn Stadium on August 22nd and 23rd.

The run started with a 2025 residency at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico and had been due to close in Europe.

Tickets go on sale August 19th at 10am local through PR Ticket, with a virtual queue opening August 18th on the artist's official site.
