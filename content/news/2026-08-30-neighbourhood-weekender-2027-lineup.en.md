Neighbourhood Weekender returns to Warrington's Victoria Park on May 29th and 30th, 2027. Paul Heaton, with guest vocalist Rianne Downey, co-headlines Saturday alongside The Reytons, while Courteeners close Sunday.

Also booked are The Vaccines, The Fratellis, The Royston Club, Gabrielle, Badly Drawn Boy and hometown act Rick Astley, plus the festival's first Haçienda Presents takeover.

General tickets go on sale at 9.30am on Friday, September 4th, after a WA1–WA5 local presale on September 2nd.
