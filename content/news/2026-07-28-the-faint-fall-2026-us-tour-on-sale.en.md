The Faint have lined up a 13-show fall US tour, opening October 30th at The Regency Live in Springfield, Missouri and wrapping November 15th at The Social in Orlando. The Saddle Creek veterans also play Indianapolis, Louisville, Memphis, Athens, Jacksonville, Miami and Tampa.

An artist presale ran July 29th through the band's site. General on-sale starts Friday, July 31st at 10 a.m. local time via Ticketmaster.
