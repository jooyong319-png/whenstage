The Camp Flog Gnaw Carnival returns to Los Angeles' Dodger Stadium on November 14th and 15th. Tyler, The Creator shares top billing with Steve Lacy, Future, Don Toliver and Turnstile.

Odd Future alumni run deep on the bill: Earl Sweatshirt, Vince Staples, Syd, Domo Genesis and more. Also booked are J.I.D, Magdalena Bay, Lupe Fiasco, Wallows, Lola Young, Isaiah Rashad and Quadeca.

Note that passes sold out before the poster even dropped.
