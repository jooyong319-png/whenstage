Tame Impala have mapped out a 2027 UK and European run, with Wet Leg opening every headline date. It opens May 28th at Manchester's Co-op Live, with two nights at London's O2 on June 1st and 2nd.

Rotterdam, Paris and Cologne arena shows follow, plus summer festival stops at Open'er, Rock Werchter and NOS Alive.

A presale opens August 26th at 9am BST for every date except Paris, which follows on August 31st. General sale is August 28th.
