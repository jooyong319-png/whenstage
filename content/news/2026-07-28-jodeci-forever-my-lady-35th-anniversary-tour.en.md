Jodeci are marking 35 years of debut album *Forever My Lady* with a 36-date US tour. It begins October 24th at The Masonic in San Francisco and finishes December 19th at Ovens Auditorium in their hometown of Charlotte, with stops in Inglewood, Phoenix, St. Louis, Detroit, Chicago, Baltimore, Houston, Nashville and Miami.

An artist presale opened July 29th. General on-sale follows Friday, July 31st at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster.
