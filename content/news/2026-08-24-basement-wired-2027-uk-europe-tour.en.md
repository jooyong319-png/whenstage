Basement will carry "WIRED," their first album in eight years, across Europe and the UK next February. The 15-date leg opens February 2nd at Amsterdam's Melkweg MAX and closes February 22nd at Dublin's National Stadium.

Fleshwater and Beton Arme support throughout. UK stops include Birmingham, Bristol, Glasgow, Norwich, London's Roundhouse, Manchester and Belfast.

An artist presale begins August 26th at 10am BST, with general sale on August 28th.
