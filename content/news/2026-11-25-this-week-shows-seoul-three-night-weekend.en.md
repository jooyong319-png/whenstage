The week after the December preview is mostly about one city. From Friday the 27th to Sunday the 29th, Seoul and Incheon run four multi-night bookings at once, and if you're flying in for one of them, you'll be sharing the subway with the other three crowds.

The biggest room belongs to [Cho Yong-pil & The Great Birth at KSPO Dome](/en/concert/en-cho-yong-pil-great-birth-seoul-20261127): 7 p.m. Friday, 6 p.m. Saturday and 5 p.m. Sunday KST, about 130 minutes each. A short walk away in the same Olympic Park, [Dynamic Duo hold their year-end shows at Olympic Hall](/en/concert/en-dynamic-duo-once-in-a-while-seoul-20261127) on the same three nights, a series that has sold out every Seoul date since 2023. Out west, [NMIXX close their first world tour at Inspire Arena in Incheon](/en/concert/en-nmixx-zero-frontier-finale-incheon-20261127), also November 27 to 29, back in the arena they sold out last year. Show times there still aren't listed, so check before you book a late flight out of the airport next door.

Saturday adds two fan events. [aespa's sixth-anniversary fan meeting](/en/concert/en-aespa-my-bogosipda-ae-fanmeeting-seoul-20261128) plays Korea University's Hwajung Gymnasium at 6 p.m. Saturday and 4 p.m. Sunday, and [Gong Yoo ends his first Asia fan meeting tour](/en/concert/en-gong-yoo-the-long-take-seoul-20261128) at Yonsei University's Grand Auditorium the same day.

Elsewhere, Thursday the 26th has [Nia Archives at Alexandra Palace](/en/concert/en-nia-archives-emotional-world-tour-london-20261126), the London stop of her Emotional World Tour. On Friday The Darkness release [Crock Of Hits](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-crock-of-hits-20261127), their first self-curated best-of, a week and a half before the arena tour starts in Glasgow. And on Sunday, [Rock The Bells Festival](/en/concert/en-rock-the-bells-festival-2026-20261129) fills the Prudential Center in Newark to close Thanksgiving weekend, with LL Cool J, SWV, Method Man & Redman, Lil' Kim, Raekwon and Ghostface Killah.

December starts on a Tuesday, and it starts busy. On the 1st, [Lord Huron open 23 Nights at the Broken Bottle at the Ryman](/en/concert/en-lord-huron-broken-bottle-nashville-20261201), the smallest room of the run, while [Syd brings the Beard Tour to Brooklyn Paramount](/en/concert/en-syd-beard-tour-brooklyn-20261201) and Kai and Sehun debut as EXO-KASE with [Waking Up The City](/en/concert/en-exo-kase-waking-up-the-city-20261201). On the 2nd, [BEAT play YouTube Theater in Inglewood](/en/concert/en-beat-fall-tour-inglewood-20261202), one night before their finale, [Amorphis and Insomnium reach the Showbox in Seattle](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-seattle-20261202), and [Malcolm Todd plays his first Seoul headline show](/en/concert/en-malcolm-todd-do-that-again-seoul-20261202) at Myunghwa Live Hall.

One thing to do tonight: general sale opens at 8 p.m. KST on Melon Ticket Global for both [EXO-KASE's SILENT CITY fan concerts](/en/concert/en-exo-kase-silent-city-fancon-seoul-20270109) in January and [THE BOYZ's ZENERATION III](/en/concert/en-the-boyz-zeneration-3-seoul-20261224) at KSPO Dome over Christmas. Same site, same minute, so pick one first.
