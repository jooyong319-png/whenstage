Iceage will bring their album For Love of Grace & the Hereafter to North America next spring. The ten-date run starts April 2nd at Seattle's Neumos and wraps April 15th at Washington DC's Black Cat.

In between come San Francisco, Los Angeles, Chicago, Detroit, Toronto, New York and Philadelphia, all club-sized rooms.

Tickets go on sale Friday, August 14th at 10 a.m. local through Ticketmaster and the band's site.
