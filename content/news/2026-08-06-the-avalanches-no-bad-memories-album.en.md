The Avalanches have dated their fourth album. No Bad Memories arrives October 16th and reportedly runs to 31 tracks, with pre-orders open now.

Robbie Chater and Tony Di Blasi built the record around advertising jingles and brand imagery from their childhoods, flipping those fragments into pop. Fourth single "Can't Get Over Losing You" features Louisiana producer Portraits of Tracy on vocals.

Earlier previews included "Together," "Every Single Weekend" with Jamie xx, and "Blue Shadows" with Karen O.
