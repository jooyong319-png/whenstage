Every ticket sale on our calendar for the next eight days happens in Korea, and every one of them happens at exactly the same moment of the day: 8 p.m. KST. For anyone buying from abroad that means 7 a.m. on the US East Coast and 4 a.m. on the West Coast, so it is worth setting the alarms now, because the week stacks up fast.

It starts tonight. [AND2BLE's first fan-con, W!nter inside](/en/concert/en-and2ble-winter-inside-fancon-seoul-20261205), opens its domestic fan club presale on Wednesday the 21st, with the global fan club round for Weverse N:DCLO (GL) members on Thursday the 22nd and the general sale on Friday the 23rd. The shows are December 5th and 6th at Korea University's Hwajung Gymnasium, and overseas buyers need to finish passport verification on Melon's global page before any of those rounds, not during them.

Thursday the 22nd is also round two for [Melon Music Awards 2026](/en/concert/en-mma-2026-20261114), the first MMA to run over two nights at Gocheok Sky Dome, on November 14th and 15th. This round is open to any current Melon pass holder, capped at two tickets per Melon ID across both rounds. No separate global sale has been announced, so for most fans outside Korea the realistic plan is the stream. One crossover worth noting: AND2BLE are on the night-two lineup, so their fans have two sales to chase in two days.

Monday the 26th is the crowded night. [NMIXX close their first world tour in Incheon](/en/concert/en-nmixx-zero-frontier-finale-incheon-20261127) with three nights at Inspire Arena from November 27th to 29th; the NSWER fan club presale runs that evening only, until 11:59 p.m., one ticket per person per show, and the general sale follows on the 27th. At the same hour, [Billlie's fifth-anniversary fan concert, The Heist Squad](/en/concert/en-billlie-the-heist-squad-fancon-seoul-20261115), opens its fan club presale for a single night at Sogang University's Mary Hall on November 15th. It is a single show, with a limit of two tickets per person.

Then the week closes on Thursday the 29th with a double: Billlie's general sale, and the general sale for [Gong Yoo's The Long Take in Seoul](/en/concert/en-gong-yoo-the-long-take-seoul-20261128), whose fan cafe presale opens on Tuesday the 27th. That show, on November 28th at Yonsei University's Grand Auditorium, is the last stop of the actor's first Asia fan meeting tour after Yokohama, Bangkok, Jakarta, Macau and Manila.

If you can only make one alarm, make it Monday.
