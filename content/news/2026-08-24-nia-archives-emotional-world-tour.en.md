Nia Archives has announced a 19-date world tour behind her Mercury Prize-shortlisted album "Emotional Junglist." Two Japanese dates in September open the run, followed in November by North American stops in Los Angeles, San Francisco, Montreal, Toronto and New York.

UK and Ireland shows land later that month, including London's Alexandra Palace, with Berlin, Amsterdam, Brussels and Paris in December and Melbourne and Sydney in January 2027.

Presale registration opens August 25th at 10am BST; general sale follows August 27th.
