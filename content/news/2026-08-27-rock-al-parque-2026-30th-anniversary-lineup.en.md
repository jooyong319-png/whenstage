Colombia's Rock al Parque has unveiled the bill for its 30th anniversary edition, running October 10th to 12th at Parque Metropolitano Simon Bolivar in Bogota.

Editors, Ministry, Mon Laferte and Apocalyptica headline a lineup of more than 60 acts, alongside Algiers, Between the Buried and Me, Darkest Hour, Stratovarius, Tokyo Ska Paradise Orchestra and a Triptykon set drawing on Hellhammer and Celtic Frost.

Entry is free, and the festival expands to four stages for the first time.
