Big Thief used their August 22nd show at Nashville's Ryman Auditorium to play a forthcoming triple album, "Horsepower," front to back. All 29 songs came from the unreleased record.

Gillian Welch, Iris DeMent and Lucinda Williams, who all feature on it, joined across the two sets and encore, alongside session players including Greg Leisz and Russ Pahl.

No release date has been confirmed. The band's Somersault Slide 360 Tour resumes in North America in September.
