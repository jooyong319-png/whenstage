American Football have added headline dates in Buenos Aires and Sao Paulo to their upcoming Latin American swing, bringing the run to five stops.

The band was already booked for Pitchfork Music Festival CDMX in Mexico City, Fauna Primavera in Santiago and a Lima appearance.

The trip supports LP4, the group's latest record. Fans in Argentina and Brazil now have their first shot at tickets.
