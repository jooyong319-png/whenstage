Four tours announced over the past week all land their general on-sales inside the same three-day stretch, July 29 through 31. They could not be more different in scale — one fills a 70,000-seat stadium, another plays a room that holds a few hundred — but if you are budgeting for fall, this is the week that decides a lot of it.

Here is what is actually going on sale, in order.


## Wednesday, July 29 — Fcukers

The New York dance-punk trio **[Fcukers](/en/concert/en-fcukers-fall-tour-grand-rapids-20260901)** open their North American headlining run on September 1 at Acrisure Amphitheater in Grand Rapids, Michigan. The band was named Spotify's Radar US Artist of 2026 on the back of their debut album *O*, and this is the first headlining tour that follows it.

The artist presale already ran on July 28; general tickets go up July 29 at 10 a.m. local time. This is the smallest-capacity run of the four, which usually means it is also the one that disappears quietest.


## Thursday, July 30 — JAY-Z

**[JAY-Z's 30th anniversary shows](/en/concert/en-jay-z-30th-anniversary-los-angeles-20261023)** reach SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles on October 23 and 24. The dates were announced July 27 alongside stadium nights at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in London on September 4 and 5 and Stade de France in Paris on September 10, and they follow three hometown shows at Yankee Stadium earlier in the month.

Presales open July 29 at 10 a.m. local, with Citi cardholder access in Los Angeles and O2 and Virgin Media early access in London. The general on-sale follows July 30 at 10 a.m. local via Ticketmaster.

Two stadium nights in a market this size means real inventory, but anniversary runs draw a different kind of demand than a standard tour cycle. The presale is where the good seats go.


## Friday, July 31 — Tyla

**[Tyla's A*POP World Tour](/en/concert/en-tyla-apop-world-tour-london-20261015)** is the largest of the four by date count: 34 shows supporting *APOP*, her second album, released July 24. The run opens October 12 at Zenith Paris and moves through Amsterdam, Glasgow, Manchester, Brussels, Berlin and Stockholm before the North American leg begins November 12 in Wheatland, California. It closes with dates in Lagos, Cape Town and Johannesburg.

The London stop at O2 Academy Brixton on October 15 is the one to watch in the UK — Brixton is a 5,000-capacity room for an artist who has been playing festival main stages all summer. Artist presale runs July 29 via Laylo, with general on-sale July 31 at 10 a.m. local through Ticketmaster.


## Friday, July 31 — Garth Brooks

**[Garth Brooks brings Blame It All On My Roots](/en/concert/en-garth-brooks-blame-it-all-roots-denver-20260904)** to Ball Arena in Denver for back-to-back nights beginning September 4. The retro-styled arena run is the most conventional of the four, and the one with the deepest built-in audience. General sale is July 31 at 10 a.m. Mountain time.


## How to prioritise

If you are choosing between them, capacity is the useful signal. Fcukers at a single amphitheater date and Tyla at Brixton are the constrained rooms; JAY-Z across two stadium nights and Brooks across two arena nights have far more seats to sell. Small rooms reward being online at 10 a.m. sharp. Stadium runs reward patience — additional dates frequently follow a strong first on-sale, as JAY-Z's own Yankee Stadium run showed earlier this month.
