Right now the biggest residency in American music is still running. Harry Styles has been at [Madison Square Garden](/en/concert/en-harry-styles-msg-residency-20260826) three nights a week since August 26, and the run goes through October 31 — thirty shows in one building, the only United States stop on his Together, Together tour this year. It is the opposite of a stadium tour: one room, one city, fans travelling to him.

What comes after it is the opposite again. Between early April and late June 2027, three very different acts are taking over North American football and baseball grounds, and the tickets for all three went on sale within about five weeks of each other this summer and fall. Looking at them side by side is a useful way to see how differently a stadium night can be built in 2027.

## Harry Styles: from one arena to the open air

The 2027 leg of [Together, Together](/en/artist/Harry%20Styles) was announced on September 10, 2026, while the Garden run was still in its second month. It follows the residency and a set of Australian dates in late 2026, and it is built around two-night stands rather than a long string of one-offs.

Three of those stands are in the data so far. The run reaches [AT&T Stadium](/en/concert/en-harry-styles-together-together-arlington-20270409) in Arlington on April 9 with CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso opening, then the [Rose Bowl](/en/concert/en-harry-styles-together-together-pasadena-20270423) in Pasadena on April 23, where Kylie Minogue takes the support slot, and [Mercedes-Benz Stadium](/en/concert/en-harry-styles-together-together-atlanta-20270521) in Atlanta on May 21. Presales started September 15 and the general sale opened September 16.

The openers are worth reading as a statement. CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso in Texas and Kylie Minogue in Pasadena are not interchangeable choices — the support bill changes city by city instead of being fixed for the whole leg, which makes each two-night stand feel a little more like its own event.

## Metallica: twelve nights, all of them in the round

Metallica confirmed twelve North American stadium dates for the M72 World Tour on September 16, 2026, with Limp Bizkit and Avatar supporting every show. The leg opens at [BC Place](/en/concert/en-metallica-m72-2027-vancouver-20270508) in Vancouver on May 8, passes through [Lucas Oil Stadium](/en/concert/en-metallica-m72-2027-indianapolis-20270605) in Indianapolis on June 5, and closes at [Rice-Eccles Stadium](/en/concert/en-metallica-m72-2027-salt-lake-city-20270619) in Salt Lake City on June 19.

The detail that matters most is a change in format. Earlier M72 stops were sold as two-night "No Repeat Weekends." In 2027, every date is a standalone concert. That makes the tour easier to attend for anyone who only wanted one night, and it means each city gets a single shot rather than a weekend.

The staging stays the same: an in-the-round stage at the 50-yard line, with the circular Snake Pit at its center, so the crowd has a 360-degree view. A portion of every ticket also goes to local charities through the band's All Within My Hands foundation. Presales opened September 21, with the general on-sale on September 25.

## Luke Combs: the earliest start and the longest reach

The first of the three to actually begin is country. Luke Combs opens the 2027 leg of his My Kinda Saturday Night Tour at [AT&T Stadium](/en/venue/AT%26T%20Stadium%2C%20Arlington%2C%20TX) on April 3 — the [opening night](/en/concert/en-luke-combs-my-kinda-saturday-night-arlington-20270403) of a twelve-date run that is also staged in the round.

His route is the widest of the three on a map: Minneapolis, Detroit, Philadelphia, Foxborough, Pittsburgh, Kansas City, Edmonton, Vancouver, Boise and Denver, before closing June 26 at Petco Park in San Diego. Treaty Oak Revival, Avery Anna, Shenandoah and Wyatt McCubbin are on the support bill. Tickets went on sale earliest, too — presales began August 19, 2026, with the general on-sale two days later on August 21.

## What the overlap tells you

Put the three schedules on one calendar and a few things stand out.

**Arlington gets two of them in one week.** Combs opens at AT&T Stadium on April 3 and Styles plays the same stadium on April 9. For Dallas–Fort Worth, early April 2027 is two stadium-sized nights at one venue less than a week apart.

**Vancouver shows up twice too.** BC Place is Metallica's opening night on May 8, and Vancouver is also one of the cities on Combs's route.

**In-the-round is no longer a novelty.** Two of the three tours are putting the stage at the center of the field. That design opens up the whole bowl and gives the crowd a view from every side, and in 2027 it reads as the default for a stadium show built to tour, not an experiment.

**June is the crowded end.** Metallica wraps on June 19 and Combs on June 26, so the last weeks of the season stack up fast.

If you are planning around any of these, the useful move is to check the city rather than the artist. The [calendar](/en) lists every date we track, and each concert page links to its source. All three tours have already been through their on-sales, so what remains now is resale and any added dates — and with Styles's Garden run ending October 31, the next announcement from that camp is the one to watch.
