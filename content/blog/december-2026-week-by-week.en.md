Most tours treat December as the place where the calendar runs out. A few treat it as the point. Read the month closely and it splits into three kinds of night: the last stop of a tour that has been on the road since early fall, a residency that plants a band in one city for days at a time, and a year-end show that only makes sense in the last weeks of the year. The clearest example of the third kind is [Yo La Tengo's eight Hanukkah nights at the Bowery Ballroom](/en/concert/en-yo-la-tengo-hanukkah-new-york-20261204), and it is a good lens for everything else below.

The other thing to know before planning around December 2026 is how much of it happens in Seoul. More than a quarter of the month's listings on WhenStage are Seoul-area dates, and they are spread across every size of room the city has.

## Week 1: December 1-6

The month opens with two very different kinds of intimacy. [Lord Huron](/en/artist/Lord%20Huron) start 23 Nights at the Broken Bottle [at the Ryman Auditorium in Nashville on the 1st](/en/concert/en-lord-huron-broken-bottle-nashville-20261201). Each show is staged inside the Broken Bottle, a desert honky-tonk from the band's fictional universe, billed as "An Evening With Lord Huron" with no opener, and the 2,362-capacity Ryman is a deliberate step down from the amphitheaters they played over the summer. The same night [Syd](/en/artist/Syd) reaches [Brooklyn Paramount](/en/concert/en-syd-beard-tour-brooklyn-20261201) as the Beard Tour turns toward the Northeast.

The 2nd is a night for tours wrapping up. [Amorphis and Insomnium](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-seattle-20261202) bring their Finnish co-headline run to The Showbox in Seattle, and BEAT — Adrian Belew, Tony Levin, Steve Vai and Terry Bozzio — play [YouTube Theater in Inglewood](/en/concert/en-beat-fall-tour-inglewood-20261202), the second-to-last date of a 40-show tour that ends in Riverside the next day. In Seoul, [Malcolm Todd plays his first headline show in the city](/en/concert/en-malcolm-todd-do-that-again-seoul-20261202) at Myunghwa Live Hall, on an Asian leg that opened in Taipei.

On the 3rd, [EsDeeKid](/en/artist/EsDeeKid) plays [OVO Arena Wembley](/en/concert/en-esdeekid-council-house-rat-london-20261203), the largest headline show of his career on a 48-date tour. Then Yo La Tengo begin their Hanukkah run on the 4th: eight consecutive nights, a surprise comedian and a surprise musical guest each evening, no song repeated across the run, proceeds to charity.

The weekend belongs to Seoul. Panic, the 1990s duo of Lee Juck and Kim Jin-pyo, play [three year-end nights at Kyung Hee University's Grand Peace Hall](/en/concert/en-panic-is-coming-again-seoul-20261204) from the 4th to the 6th, following their April reunion shows. On the 5th, [Khalid plays KINTEX in Goyang](/en/concert/en-khalid-always-summer-somewhere-seoul-20261205), his first headline concert in Korea in eight years, while [YUURI moves up to Inspire Arena in Incheon](/en/concert/en-yuuri-live-2026-incheon-20261205) for his largest Korean show to date.

## Week 2: December 7-13

The busiest week of December 2026 starts with shoegaze in Seoul and an arena run across the UK. [Slowdive play KBS Arena in Seoul on the 8th](/en/concert/en-slowdive-live-2026-seoul-20261208), and the same night [The Darkness](/en/artist/The%20Darkness) open the Best Of The Darkness tour [at the OVO Hydro in Glasgow](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-glasgow-20261208) — the first of seven arena dates and their largest headline run in twenty years. Leeds follows on the 9th, then [Manchester's AO Arena on the 11th](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-manchester-20261211), Birmingham on the 12th and Cardiff on the 13th. Brothers Osborne and A support every night.

In the US, the Jonas Brothers bring their rebuilt 2008 tour to [Ball Arena in Denver on the 9th](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-burning-up-all-over-again-denver-20261209). Goose stage their 13th Goosemas [at Jim Whelan Boardwalk Hall in Atlantic City](/en/concert/en-goose-goosemas-atlantic-city-20261211) on the 11th and 12th, the first time the celebration has gone to Atlantic City, with a dollar from every ticket going to the Western Sun Foundation. The 11th is also the release date for Iron Maiden's [fiftieth-anniversary vinyl box](/en/concert/en-iron-maiden-50th-anniversary-box-set-20261211), collecting their first nine studio albums on individually coloured records.

Back in Seoul, the 12th offers two extremes. [DAVICHI return to KSPO Dome](/en/concert/en-davichi-in-our-scenes-seoul-20261212) for the first time in 17 years, staging two nights in the round with all 360 degrees open to seating. The same evening, [DOMi & JD BECK play their first Seoul headline show](/en/concert/en-domi-jd-beck-who-asked-seoul-20261212) at Gonggam Hall, a much smaller room for a live act built around improvisation.

## Week 3: December 14-20

This is the week of finales. The Darkness reach [the Brighton Centre on the 15th](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-brighton-20261215) and close the tour [at The O2 in London on December 16](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-london-20261216), their first headline show at the venue. The same night Syd ends the Beard Tour [at The Wiltern in Los Angeles](/en/concert/en-syd-beard-tour-los-angeles-20261216), a hometown close to a 23-show run that started in Vancouver.

Lord Huron's residency reaches [Kings Theatre in Brooklyn on the 18th](/en/concert/en-lord-huron-broken-bottle-brooklyn-20261218), the first of two nights. In Seoul, [wave to earth](/en/artist/wave%20to%20earth) wrap the ( ) pieces tour with [three hometown nights at Korea University's Hwajung Gymnasium](/en/concert/en-wave-to-earth-the-pieces-tour-seoul-20261218), December 18 to 20. Every seat went at the first booking, the 18th was added afterwards, and that sold out too.

The 19th stacks three endings and a beginning. Amorphis and Insomnium finish [at Irving Plaza in New York](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-new-york-20261219), the Jonas Brothers play [UBS Arena at Belmont Park](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-burning-up-all-over-again-belmont-park-20261219), and [Trans-Siberian Orchestra](/en/artist/Trans-Siberian%20Orchestra) play two shows [at PPG Paints Arena in Pittsburgh](/en/concert/en-trans-siberian-orchestra-pittsburgh-20261219), then two more [in Philadelphia on the 20th](/en/concert/en-trans-siberian-orchestra-philadelphia-20261220). Their 30th-anniversary winter run splits into eastern and western casts to cover about fifty cities before December 30.

## Week 4: December 21-31

The Jonas Brothers close their 45-stop arena run [at Prudential Center in Newark on December 21](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-prudential-center-20261221). Then the calendar goes quiet until two very different holiday shows. In Seoul, [J.Y. Park](/en/artist/J.Y.%20Park) plays [three nights at Olympic Hall](/en/concert/en-jy-park-wet-seoul-20261224) from Christmas Eve, named after WET, the summer single that ended his six-year break from seasonal releases.

And on December 31, [Backstreet Boys see in 2027 at Sphere in Las Vegas](/en/concert/en-backstreet-boys-into-the-millennium-nye-las-vegas-20261231), with further shows on January 1 and 2. The Into The Millennium residency has already drawn more than 750,000 people across 47 sold-out nights.

## How to use the month

If you are choosing one trip, the pattern is clear: December rewards the final night. Tour closers in London, Los Angeles, New York and Newark tend to be the shows bands remember, and the residencies — Lord Huron, Yo La Tengo, Goose — reward anyone who can be in one city for a few days.
