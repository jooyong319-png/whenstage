Most of the concert calendar runs on novelty: a new album, a new tour name, a new set of cities. The last stretch of 2026 works differently. Scan November into the first weeks of January and the through-line is repetition — annual traditions returning for their thirteenth year, benefit residencies that have outlived the venues around them, sequels to tours that only launched months earlier, and albums played front to back because the calendar says they turned twenty-five. These are shows built for people who already came last time.

Here is how the back end of the year breaks down.

## The annual rituals

Nothing illustrates the pattern better than [Goosemas](/en/concert/en-goose-goosemas-atlantic-city-20261211). The Connecticut jam band [Goose](/en/artist/Goose) have been staging their own year-end celebration since long before they were filling arenas, and the 2026 edition — the thirteenth — moves to [Jim Whelan Boardwalk Hall](/en/venue/Jim%20Whelan%20Boardwalk%20Hall%2C%20Atlantic%20City%2C%20NJ) in Atlantic City for the first time, across two nights on December 11 and 12. General tickets went on sale July 24, after VIP, travel-package and local presales had already moved through the fanbase, which tells you most of what you need to know about how these events are actually sold. One dollar from every ticket goes to the Western Sun Foundation, which funds grassroots nonprofits in the cities the band tours.

A week earlier and a hundred and thirty miles north, [Yo La Tengo](/en/artist/Yo%20La%20Tengo) begin [eight consecutive nights of their Hanukkah benefit residency](/en/concert/en-yo-la-tengo-hanukkah-new-york-20261204) at the [Bowery Ballroom](/en/venue/Bowery%20Ballroom%2C%20New%20York%2C%20NY), running December 4 through December 11. The format has barely changed in decades: a surprise comedian and a surprise musical guest each night, proceeds to charity, and a Yo La Tengo set that repeats no song across the entire run — usually landing on an all-covers encore. Eight nights without a repeated song is the sort of constraint a band only takes on because the audience is the same audience, night after night, year after year. Tickets went on sale July 24.

## Anniversaries, played front to back

The full-album show has become its own genre, and November 7 produces an unusually clean example: [Jimmy Eat World and The Format co-headline the Hollywood Bowl](/en/concert/en-jimmy-eat-world-the-format-hollywood-bowl-20261107), each playing a record in its entirety. Jimmy Eat World take on *Bleed American* for its twenty-fifth anniversary — *Sweetness*, *The Middle*, *Hear You Me* — while The Format perform 2006's *Dog Problems* front to back for its twentieth.

Two details make this more than a nostalgia billing. The first is that these two Arizona bands, whose careers ran parallel through the same scene, have never actually shared a stage before. The second is scarcity: it is one of only two full-album shows The Format have scheduled all year. A one-off pairing built for a single night at the Bowl is a strange, specific thing to assemble, and it exists because both catalogs hit round numbers at the same moment.

## The fall tours that spill into November

Not everything in this window is ceremonial. Several fall routes simply run long enough to land here.

[Quadeca](/en/concert/en-quadeca-life-1-tour-seattle-20261106) plays The Showbox in Seattle on November 6, one of 23 North American dates behind his fifth album *Life 1*. The run opened October 10 in Mesa, Arizona and closes November 10 at The Fonda in his native Los Angeles, with Derby and Oleka opening throughout; a portion of proceeds goes to the cancer research nonprofit City of Hope.

[Silverstein and Story of the Year](/en/concert/en-silverstein-story-of-the-year-camp-screamo-2-seattle-20261116) launch Camp Screamo 2 at Showbox SoDo in Seattle on November 16 — the same city, ten days later, at the venue's larger sibling. The co-headlining run covers 24 North American dates through December 13 at Citizens House of Blues in Boston, with Stand Atlantic and Footballhead supporting throughout. The name is the point: this is a second leg of a package that already toured earlier in the year, turned around fast enough to still be the same tour.

[The Faint](/en/concert/en-the-faint-fall-tour-springfield-20261030) are working smaller rooms on the same timeline, a 13-date club run that opens October 30 in Springfield, Missouri and winds through Indianapolis, Louisville, Memphis, Athens and Miami before finishing November 15 at The Social in Orlando. And [THAO](/en/concert/en-thao-show-me-20261030) releases *Show Me* on October 30, her first album in six years and her return to Kill Rock Stars, co-produced with Merrill Garbus and Nate Brenner of Tune-Yards and featuring Neko Case, The Linda Lindas and Black Pumas guitarist Adrian Quesada. Short West Coast and East Coast headline dates follow in November.

## Abroad, and then January

The traditions are not only American. [EsDeeKid](/en/concert/en-esdeekid-council-house-rat-london-20261203) brings the Council House Rat Tour to OVO Arena Wembley on December 3 — the biggest headline show of the Liverpool rapper's career to date, and one stop on a 48-date trek that opens with 28 North American shows from September, crosses to Europe in November, and includes Dublin, Glasgow and two homecoming nights in Liverpool.

Then the year turns and the format gets stranger. [John Mulaney](/en/concert/en-john-mulaney-takes-manhattan-msg-20270115) closes Mulaney Takes Manhattan at Madison Square Garden on January 15, 2027 — the ninth night of a nine-night residency staged in nine different New York City rooms. It starts January 7 at the Comedy Cellar and climbs deliberately in scale through the Blue Note, Joe's Pub, the Bowery Ballroom, the Music Box Theatre, the Beacon Theatre, Carnegie Hall and Radio City before reaching the Garden. Same performer, same city, nine escalating rooms in nine nights: a residency structured as a staircase rather than a stand. The dates extend his Mister Whatever world tour, which runs across North America through December 2026.

## What to take from it

If you are planning the rest of the year, the practical lesson is that this stretch rewards early attention rather than fast fingers. The Goose and Yo La Tengo runs both went on sale back in late July, months out, because the people buying them already knew the date. The anniversary shows are one-offs by construction. The fall tours are the only part of the window that behaves like a normal on-sale.
