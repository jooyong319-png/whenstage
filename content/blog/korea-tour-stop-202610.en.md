For most of the past decade, a Korean date was something a world tour added late, if at all — one arena night squeezed between Tokyo and Manila. That is no longer what the calendar looks like. Between October 2026 and May 2027, Post Malone, The Weeknd, Charlie Puth, Benson Boone, 5 Seconds of Summer, Khalid, My Chemical Romance, Maroon 5 and Bruno Mars all have headline dates in Korea, and several of them are stadium shows rather than arena stops.

There is a detail in all of this that gets flattened whenever these shows are announced in English. They are sold as Seoul concerts. Almost none of them are actually in Seoul.

## One suburban ground, four stadium headliners

[Post Malone opens the run at Goyang Stadium](/en/concert/en-post-malone-big-stadium-seoul-20261002) on October 2, 2026, his first Korean headline show since 2023, in a bowl that holds roughly 40,000. Five days later [The Weeknd plays the same field](/en/concert/en-the-weeknd-after-hours-til-dawn-seoul-20261007) for two nights, October 7 and 8, with Japanese duo Creepy Nuts opening and the run billed locally as Hyundai Card Super Concert 28. Then [Charlie Puth takes it on October 11](/en/concert/en-charlie-puth-whatevers-clever-seoul-20261011) for the first stadium show of his career in the country, behind *Whatever's Clever!*, with a medley built specifically for the Korean audience.

Three stadium headliners in ten days, all at [the same venue](/en/venue/Goyang%20Stadium%2C%20Goyang) — which sits in Gyeonggi Province, outside the capital. The reason is structural rather than curatorial: the city itself has no large stadium routinely available for international pop, so the shows that are marketed as Seoul dates land in Goyang. [Bruno Mars](/en/artist/Bruno%20Mars) makes the point emphatically next spring with [six nights at the same stadium](/en/concert/en-bruno-mars-the-romantic-seoul-20270521) in May 2027 — May 21, 22, 25, 26, 29 and 30 — with Anderson .Paak appearing as DJ Pee .Wee.

## The convention centre doing arena work

The tier below stadium scale ends up somewhere stranger. KINTEX in Goyang is an exhibition complex, and four touring acts book its halls over four months. [Benson Boone plays Hall 10 on October 26](/en/concert/en-benson-boone-wanted-man-seoul-20261026), his second Korean headline date ever. [5 Seconds of Summer take Hall 1 on November 19](/en/concert/en-5sos-everyones-a-star-seoul-20261119) for their first standalone show in the country and their first appearance since the 2017 Pentaport Rock Festival — all standing, 143,000 won up to a 320,000 won VIP package. [Khalid returns to Hall 10 on December 5](/en/concert/en-khalid-always-summer-somewhere-seoul-20261205) after eight years away, and [Maroon 5 spread across Halls 4 and 5 on January 27, 2027](/en/concert/en-maroon-5-asia-2027-seoul-20270127).

Read as a group, these are not legacy acts collecting a payday. They are mid-cycle tours treating Korea as a routine leg, in rooms built for trade fairs because the purpose-built alternatives are booked or too small.

## Incheon gets the rest

The third cluster sits west, near the airport. [My Chemical Romance play Paradise City in Incheon on November 7](/en/concert/en-mcr-black-parade-2026-incheon-20261107) — their first Korean concert in roughly eighteen years, performing *The Black Parade* in full for its twentieth anniversary. The date was originally set for April 2026 and moved, with existing tickets carried over rather than reissued. [EDC Korea returns on October 3 and 4](/en/concert/en-edc-korea-2026-20261003) at the Inspire Entertainment Resort, more than 70 acts across four stages behind Tiësto, DJ Snake, Fisher and Alok. [DAY6 hold a three-day fan meeting at Inspire Arena](/en/concert/en-day6-5th-fanmeeting-incheon-20261023) from October 23, with the final day streamed worldwide.

## Traffic in the other direction

The same months show the export side of the arrangement, and that traffic does start inside the city. [&TEAM close their BLAZE THE WAY tour at KSPO Dome](/en/concert/en-andteam-blaze-the-way-encore-seoul-20261003) in Olympic Park on October 3 and 4, becoming the first Japanese boy group to headline solo dome shows in both Korea and Japan. [TOMORROW X TOGETHER launch STEAL THE WIND with three KSPO Dome nights](/en/concert/en-txt-steal-the-wind-seoul-20261113) from November 13 before the tour moves through Asia, North America and Europe — the home dates function as the opening, not the victory lap.

## If you are buying from abroad

A few practical things repeat across these listings. Ticketing splits between NOL (formerly Interpark) and Live Nation Korea, both of which run English-language booking pages for overseas buyers, and global sales often use a separate page with its own verification window — TOMORROW X TOGETHER required membership checks in late September for an early October presale. Korean rules also cap some stadium shows at ages 19 and over, which applies to both the Post Malone and The Weeknd dates. And if a listing says Seoul, check the actual address before booking a hotel: Goyang, Incheon and Olympic Park are three genuinely different commutes.
