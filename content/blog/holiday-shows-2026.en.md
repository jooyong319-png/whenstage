Most tours are built around an album and routed around a map. A holiday tour is built around a date. It has a hard expiry — nobody buys a ticket to a Christmas show on December 28 — and that single constraint changes almost everything else about it: how many shows fit into a day, which rooms get booked, and who is in the audience. Looking across the next eight weeks of the calendar, the seasonal shows fall into three fairly distinct shapes, and knowing which one you are buying into tells you a lot about the night you will get.

## The production built only for this season

The purest version of the holiday show is the one that would not exist in any other month. [Trans-Siberian Orchestra](/en/artist/Trans-Siberian%20Orchestra) is the long-running example. This year's winter tour opens in Green Bay, and two days later the group plays [Ball Arena in Denver twice on Saturday, November 21](/en/concert/en-trans-siberian-orchestra-denver-20261121), at 3 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. local time. The first set restages The Ghosts of Christmas Eve to mark 30 years since the debut album Christmas Eve and Other Stories, and a second set of holiday material follows with lasers and pyrotechnics.

The matinee-plus-evening format is the tell. A normal arena tour plays one show a night and travels; a holiday production has a fixed window, so it doubles up. The pattern repeats late in the run: [PPG Paints Arena in Pittsburgh on December 19](/en/concert/en-trans-siberian-orchestra-pittsburgh-20261219), with a 2:30 p.m. matinee and a 7:30 p.m. show, then [Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia on December 20](/en/concert/en-trans-siberian-orchestra-philadelphia-20261220), at 3 p.m. and 7:30 p.m. The tour ends December 30 in St. Louis. If you are bringing children or older relatives, the afternoon slot is the one this format is designed around.

[Pentatonix](/en/artist/Pentatonix) works on the same clock with a different instrument — none at all. The a cappella group opens the 2026 leg of its Christmas in the City Tour [at the Tacoma Dome on November 5](/en/concert/en-pentatonix-christmas-in-the-city-tacoma-20261105), the first of 28 North American arena dates across November and December. The tour supports the deluxe edition of the group's 2025 holiday album Christmas in the City, a New York-inspired record that leaned on jazz arrangements of standards alongside original songs. That makes it one of the few holiday tours tied to an actual release cycle rather than to a catalog.

## The theatrical version

[Lindsey Stirling](/en/artist/Lindsey%20Stirling) sits between a concert and a stage show. Her 25-city Snow Waltz Holiday Tour launches [at Hard Rock Live in Hollywood, Florida on November 18](/en/concert/en-lindsey-stirling-snow-waltz-hollywood-20261118), pairing her violin with dance and staging built around holiday material. The route works north and west through Syracuse, Philadelphia, St. Louis and Tucson, includes a three-night stand in Las Vegas, and finishes in Texas on December 23. Electroacoustic duo ARKAI supports every date. A December 23 finale is about as late as a seasonal run can reasonably go — the date itself is the closing curtain.

## Seoul's version: Christmas week is concert week

In Korea the seasonal show looks less like a themed production and more like a reserved slot on the calendar. Artists book the days around Christmas and New Year's Eve the way Western acts book a hometown arena. Two of these go on sale this week, which makes them the most time-sensitive items in this piece.

[N.Flying](/en/artist/N.Flying) play three shows of 'Once Upon a Snowball' [at Korea University's Hwajung Gymnasium](/en/concert/en-n-flying-once-upon-a-snowball-seoul-20261224): December 24 at 7:30 p.m., December 25 at 5 p.m. and December 26 at 4 p.m. KST. Melon Ticket sells exclusively. Fifth-generation N.Fia fan club members buy on November 4 at 8 p.m. KST, and the general sale opens November 6 at 8 p.m. KST. All seats are 143,000 won, two per show, and fans who verified on Melon's global page must also book there.

[J.Y. Park](/en/artist/J.Y.%20Park) takes the same three days at Olympic Hall: [December 24 at 8 p.m., then December 25 and 26 at 5 p.m.](/en/concert/en-jy-park-wet-seoul-20261224) The year-end concert is named after WET, the summer single that ended his six-year break from seasonal releases.

The New Year's slot belongs to [Sung Si-kyung](/en/artist/Sung%20Si-kyung), who rings in 2027 with [four shows at KSPO Dome](/en/concert/en-sung-si-kyung-year-end-concert-seoul-20261231) — December 31 at 9 p.m., January 1 at 7 p.m., and January 2 and 3 at 6 p.m. KST. The NOL Ticket fan club presale for PURPLEOCEAN KOREA and PURPLEOCEAN JAPAN members opens November 6 at 8 p.m. KST, with the general sale on November 12 at 8 p.m. KST. Overseas fans can book on NOL World, where seats run from 121,000 to 165,000 won.

## The other holiday traditions

Not every seasonal show is about Christmas. [Yo La Tengo](/en/artist/Yo%20La%20Tengo) return to the Bowery Ballroom for [eight consecutive Hanukkah nights from December 4 to 11](/en/concert/en-yo-la-tengo-hanukkah-new-york-20261204), a benefit run that repeats no song across the residency. [Goose](/en/artist/Goose) take their 13th annual Goosemas to [Atlantic City's Boardwalk Hall on December 11 and 12](/en/concert/en-goose-goosemas-atlantic-city-20261211). And [Backstreet Boys](/en/artist/Backstreet%20Boys) close the year with a [New Year's Eve show at Sphere in Las Vegas](/en/concert/en-backstreet-boys-into-the-millennium-nye-las-vegas-20261231), followed by January 1 and 2.

The practical takeaway: a holiday show cannot move to a later date, so the whole run is squeezed into a few weeks. If one of these is on your list, the on-sale dates above matter more than they would for an ordinary tour — and the full winter calendar is always on the [WhenStage calendar](/en).
