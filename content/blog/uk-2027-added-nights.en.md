Most of the shows announced this summer for 2027 have something in common that is easy to miss if you only read the headline dates: the booking that went on sale is not the booking that was originally planned. Across the UK and Ireland, on-sale after on-sale in July, August and September 2026 ended with promoters bolting extra nights onto runs that were supposed to be finished. It is a pattern worth reading on its own, because it says more about where demand actually sits than any single announcement does.

Here is what that looks like across the next ten months of British and Irish dates.

## Eleven nights in one room

The clearest case is [Olivia Rodrigo's London residency](/en/concert/en-olivia-rodrigo-unraveled-tour-london-20270405), which opens at The O2 on April 5, 2027. It is the only British stop on The Unraveled Tour — a UK-exclusive stand rather than a leg — and demand pushed the booking out to eleven nights, running through April and closing the entire world tour on May 10, 2027. Grace Ives supports the London shows. Tickets first moved through an O2 priority presale back in May 2026, well before the general on-sale, which is part of why the run kept growing rather than simply selling out.

Ireland produced the rarer version of the same story. [Fontaines D.C. headline Slane Castle](/en/concert/en-fontaines-dc-slane-castle-20270626) in County Meath on June 26, 2027 — the largest show of the Dublin band's career, at a site with capacity for roughly 80,000, and only the third time an Irish act has headlined Slane, after U2 and Thin Lizzy. Hayley Williams, Damien Dempsey and Madra Salach are among the announced guests. Then, on September 2, 2026, [a second Slane date was added](/en/concert/en-fontaines-dc-slane-castle-20270627) for Sunday June 27. Slane normally hosts one concert a year. Back-to-back nights there are close to unheard of.

[Noah Kahan](/en/artist/Noah%20Kahan) makes the jump to stadiums in the same stretch. His [UK and Ireland run](/en/concert/en-noah-kahan-last-of-the-bugs-glasgow-20270713) opens at Hampden Park in Glasgow on July 13, 2027, reaches Dublin's Aviva Stadium on July 17, and finishes at [Wembley Stadium](/en/venue/Wembley%20Stadium%2C%20London) on July 23 — the biggest headline show of his career, with a second Wembley night added on July 24 after demand. The shows follow *The Great Divide*, his second UK number one album.

## The same squeeze in much smaller rooms

This is not only a stadium phenomenon, which is what makes it interesting. [Supergrass](/en/artist/Supergrass) booked a thirteen-date spring tour running from Newcastle on April 26 to Portsmouth on May 12, and ended up adding three shows to it. They play [two consecutive nights at Glasgow's Barrowland Ballroom](/en/concert/en-supergrass-glasgow-20270428) on April 27 and 28, [two at Manchester Academy](/en/concert/en-supergrass-manchester-20270501) on May 1 and 2, and [a three-night London residency](/en/concert/en-supergrass-london-20270507) at the O2 Forum Kentish Town from May 7, the third date added after the first two sold out. Gaz Coombes has said the band are moving on from the *I Should Coco* anniversary shows and pulling hits and deep cuts from across all six albums instead — a full-catalogue tour rather than a nostalgia package, sold to rooms of two or three thousand.

## Returns measured in years

The other thread through the UK calendar is artists coming back after unusually long gaps. [Megadeth play OVO Arena Wembley on March 11, 2027](/en/concert/en-megadeth-breakout-hibernation-london-20270311), their first British headline dates in eleven years, on the European chapter of a multi-year farewell that runs twenty-four arena dates from Belfast on March 9 to Lisbon on April 13, with Black Label Society and Testament throughout. [Thomas Rhett's three-date run](/en/concert/en-thomas-rhett-soundtrack-to-life-london-20270423) — Manchester on April 22, London on April 23, Glasgow on April 25 — is his first headline arena tour in the UK in nearly a decade.

[Ride](/en/artist/Ride) return to [O2 Academy Brixton on February 13, 2027](/en/concert/en-ride-here-and-now-london-20270213), their first live shows in three years, in the same room they filmed a concert video in 1992. [Basement](/en/concert/en-basement-wired-tour-london-20270218) headline the Roundhouse on February 18 behind *WIRED*, their first album in more than eight years. And [Jake Bugg](/en/artist/Jake%20Bugg) closes a [stripped-back solo tour](/en/concert/en-jake-bugg-alamo-tour-london-20270226) at Union Chapel on February 26, ten dates that begin at Manchester's Albert Hall on February 6.

## Before any of it: December

The UK winter fills in first. [The Darkness](/en/artist/The%20Darkness) open a seven-date arena run — their largest in twenty years — [at the OVO Hydro in Glasgow on December 8](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-glasgow-20261208) and [close at The O2 on December 16](/en/concert/en-the-darkness-london-20261216), their first headline show at the venue, following the greatest-hits collection *Crock Of Hits*. Liverpool rapper [EsDeeKid](/en/artist/EsDeeKid) plays the biggest headline show of his career at [OVO Arena Wembley on December 3](/en/concert/en-esdeekid-council-house-rat-london-20261203), and [aespa](/en/concert/en-aespa-synk-complaexity-london-20270116) bring a rebuilt SYNK production to The O2 on January 16.

Later in the year the calendar widens again: [Tame Impala](/en/artist/Tame%20Impala) launch a UK and European tour at Co-op Live in Manchester on May 28 before [three nights at The O2](/en/concert/en-tame-impala-deadbeat-london-20270601) from June 1 with Wet Leg, [Weezer](/en/artist/Weezer) reach [Alexandra Palace on May 22](/en/concert/en-weezer-the-gathering-london-20270522), and [Neighbourhood Weekender](/en/concert/en-neighbourhood-weekender-2027-20270529) announced its Warrington lineup unusually early, with Paul Heaton and The Reytons co-headlining the Saturday and Courteeners closing the Sunday.

The practical lesson for anyone buying: for UK dates right now, the first announcement is a floor, not a ceiling. Several of these runs grew by a night or three within days of going on sale, and the added dates were often the easiest tickets in the whole run.
