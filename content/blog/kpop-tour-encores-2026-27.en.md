In most of the touring world, the last night of a tour is simply the last date on the poster. In K-pop it is usually a separate event with its own name, its own ticket sale and its own poster: the encore. Between late November 2026 and the end of January 2027, three big tours close this way in Seoul and Incheon, and if you are thinking about flying in for one of them, it helps to understand what you are actually buying.

## The shape of the format

The pattern is consistent. A tour opens in Seoul, travels through Asia, Europe or North America for months, and then comes home for a short run billed as an encore or a finale, usually in the same city and often in the same room where it began. The homecoming is announced late, sold as its own event, and treated by fans as the definitive version of the show, the one where the production has been refined by dozens of nights abroad.

That is why these dates sell differently from the opening run. The fan club presale matters more, ticket limits are tighter, and overseas buyers are funneled through specific global booking pages.

## NMIXX: back to the arena that sold out

[NMIXX close their first world tour, Episode 1: Zero Frontier, at Inspire Arena](/en/concert/en-nmixx-zero-frontier-finale-incheon-20261127) with three nights from November 27 to 29, 2026. The choice of room is the story here: it is the arena where their two November 2025 shows sold out, and they return to it after a run that took in Europe, North America and Asia. The finale was sold on NOL, with the NSWER 4th-generation fan club presale limited to one ticket per person per show. Show times had not been listed at the time of writing. For travelers, [Inspire Arena](/en/venue/Inspire%20Arena%2C%20Incheon) is about 14 minutes by car from Incheon Airport Terminal 1, which makes it one of the easiest major K-pop rooms to reach straight off a flight.

## IVE: a three-night encore at KSPO Dome

The biggest of the season is [IVE's SHOW WHAT I AM encore at KSPO Dome](/en/concert/en-ive-show-what-i-am-encore-seoul-20270129) on January 29, 30 and 31, 2027. Their second world tour began at the same Olympic Park venue in October 2025, so this is a full circle in the most literal sense. The three shows start at different times — 7:30 p.m., 6 p.m. and 4 p.m. KST — which is worth noticing if you are trying to fit a Sunday afternoon show around a flight home.

The ticketing details are the practical part. Sales run only through Melon Ticket, and overseas fans should use the Melon Ticket Global page, where the DIVE fan club presale runs December 17, one day after the Korean page. Fan club verification on the global page is open December 10 to 17, and general sale opens December 18 at 8 p.m. KST. VIP seats at 198,000 won include a pre-show soundcheck event; regular seats are 154,000 won. The presale is limited to one ticket per show, the general sale to two, and the show runs about 120 minutes with no intermission.

## Byeon Woo-seok: the encore as a fan meeting

The format is not only for music acts. Actor [Byeon Woo-seok](/en/artist/Byeon%20Woo-seok) wraps his Secret Library Asia fan meeting tour with [a single encore night at Korea University's Hwajung Gymnasium](/en/concert/en-byeon-woo-seok-secret-library-encore-seoul-20261230) on December 30 at 7 p.m. KST. The tour started in Seoul in July and went on to Bangkok, Yokohama, Taipei, Singapore, Manila, Jakarta and Hong Kong. Because it is one night only, the rules are strict: one ticket per Melon Ticket ID across both rounds, all floor tickets and global-page bookings collected on site with ID checks, and no transfers. Seats are 110,000 won, or 99,000 won for restricted view.

## The fall already showed how this works

This winter's run follows a busy autumn of homecomings. [&TEAM closed BLAZE THE WAY at KSPO Dome](/en/concert/en-andteam-blaze-the-way-encore-seoul-20261003) on October 3 and 4 after announcing the dates as a surprise during an earlier Incheon show. [ILLIT](/en/artist/ILLIT) played two encore nights at Inspire Arena on October 17 and 18 after a first tour that sold out every stop. The band LUCY ran [their ISLAND encore](/en/concert/en-lucy-island-encore-seoul-20261023) from October 23 to 25, and used it to open a short Asian leg. [MONSTA X](/en/artist/MONSTA%20X) closed THE X : NEXUS in Incheon on November 21 and 22, with both nights also carried as a paid online stream.

That last detail is a useful reminder: if you cannot get a seat, a homecoming show is often the date most likely to be streamed.

## How the West does it instead

Compare that with the American calendar, where the closing night is rarely branded as a separate event. The Jonas Brothers' [Burning Up Tour All Over Again ends at Prudential Center in Newark](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-prudential-center-20261221) on December 21, simply as the final stop of 45 arena dates. [Lord Huron](/en/artist/Lord%20Huron) finish 23 Nights at the Broken Bottle at [The Wiltern](/en/venue/The%20Wiltern%2C%20Los%20Angeles%2C%20CA) in their home city of Los Angeles on January 11. [John Mulaney](/en/artist/John%20Mulaney) turns the idea into a nine-room climb across New York that ends at Madison Square Garden on January 15. A homecoming exists in both cultures, but only in Korea is it standard practice to sell it as its own show.

For fans outside Korea, that distinction is the takeaway: the encore is the night to plan a trip around, and it is also the hardest ticket of the tour. Check the [calendar](/en) for on-sale dates, and read the [guide](/en/guide) before your first Korean ticketing attempt.
