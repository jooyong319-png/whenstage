Two things are happening in metal between September 2026 and early 2027, and they are pulling in opposite directions. A set of long-running institutions are closing up: farewell tours, anniversary box sets, last studio albums. At the same time, a different set of bands are releasing tenth albums, first albums in over a decade, and first solo records in 21 years. Put the two lists side by side and the season reads less like a genre in decline than one shedding a layer.

## The goodbyes are real ones

[Ministry](/en/artist/Ministry) open their U.S. farewell dates at [Red Rocks Amphitheatre](/en/venue/Red%20Rocks%20Amphitheatre%2C%20Morrison%2C%20CO) on [September 15](/en/concert/en-ministry-hate-to-go-farewell-red-rocks-20260915), and after 45 years founder Al Jourgensen is retiring the band outright. The run is tied to a final studio album, *Hate To Go (Take Out or Delivery)*, due October 30, and continues through Louder Than Life, Sick New World Texas and a Chicago headline show at the Salt Shed on October 3 before a Goodbye Europe leg in spring 2027. Industrial metal never had many bands that lasted this long; it had Ministry.

Megadeth's exit is slower and larger. [Breakout: Hibernation Of The Nations](/en/concert/en-megadeth-breakout-hibernation-london-20270311) is the European chapter of a multi-year farewell, a 24-date arena run opening March 9, 2027 in Belfast and ending April 13 in Lisbon, with OVO Arena Wembley on March 11. Black Label Society and Testament support throughout, and these are the band's first British headline dates in eleven years — a farewell tour that had to schedule a homecoming because the ordinary touring cycle stopped including one.

The catalog end of the goodbye is [Iron Maiden](/en/artist/Iron%20Maiden)'s fiftieth-anniversary [vinyl box set](/en/concert/en-iron-maiden-50th-anniversary-box-set-20261211), out December 11, collecting the first nine albums from the 1980 debut through *Fear Of The Dark*, each pressed on its own colour. It is a monument, and monuments get built when the building is finished.

## The other half of the season is new work

[Tony Iommi](/en/artist/Tony%20Iommi) releases [*From the Dark*](/en/concert/en-tony-iommi-from-the-dark-20261023) on October 23 — his first solo album in 21 years and his first full-length of any kind since Black Sabbath's *13* in 2013. Eight tracks, produced with Mike Exeter, with Jorn Lande on vocals. The man who wrote heavy metal's founding riff is putting out new material while his contemporaries pack up.

A month later, New Orleans metal group [Down](/en/artist/Down) return with [*Volume V*](/en/concert/en-down-volume-v-20261120) on November 20, their first full-length in twelve years, led by a single on which Phil Anselmo trades vocals with King Diamond. And [Wolves in the Throne Room](/en/artist/Wolves%20in%20the%20Throne%20Room) release [*Estuary*](/en/concert/en-wolves-in-the-throne-room-estuary-20261023), a tenth album recorded largely at their own Owl Lodge studio with drums tracked in a converted church in Anacortes, Washington. Nobody in black metal is obliged to reach a tenth record; most bands in that world dissolve well before it.

## Two Finnish metal bands turn a release into a tour

The neatest piece of routing this season belongs to [Insomnium](/en/artist/Insomnium), whose tenth album [*Netherworlds*](/en/concert/en-insomnium-netherworlds-20261016) arrives October 16, and who then spend the next two months onstage behind it. The 23-date North American co-headline tour with fellow Finns Amorphis opens November 17 at [Paradise Rock Club](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-boston-20261117) in Boston, reaches [The Showbox](/en/venue/The%20Showbox%2C%20Seattle%2C%20WA) in Seattle on [December 2](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-seattle-20261202) and finishes at [Irving Plaza](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-new-york-20261219) in New York on December 19. The two split headline duties rather than ranking themselves, with Texas melodic death metal band Hinayana supporting the whole run.

Swedish melodic death metal veterans [Amon Amarth](/en/artist/Amon%20Amarth) do the same thing on the other side of the Atlantic. The Allfather Awakens tour opens October 9 at the [O2 Apollo](/en/concert/en-amon-amarth-o2-apollo-manchester-20261009) in Manchester with an all-Swedish bill — Orbit Culture and Soilwork supporting — then runs roughly 28 dates through Europe to a November 15 finish in Lisbon, carrying a new theatrical stage production and an extended set.

## What the shape tells you

Farewells cluster because the generation that built arena-scale metal in the 1980s is reaching the same age at the same time, and a farewell tour is now a product category of its own. What is less predictable is the second column: tenth albums from bands who were supposed to be niche, comebacks after twelve-year gaps, a founding guitarist recording again at 78. The genre is not being handed down so much as continuing in two registers at once. If you want the full picture, the [calendar](/en) has every one of these dates, and early 2027 already has [System of a Down and Faith No More](/en/concert/en-system-of-a-down-faith-no-more-sydney-20270122) playing four stadiums across Australia and New Zealand from January 22 — Faith No More's first shows in nearly a decade.
