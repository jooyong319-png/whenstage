October gets the headlines because the stadium runs peak there. November is the harder month to read, and the more interesting one. It is where fall tours that opened in September finally reach their last cities, where the holiday circuit quietly starts a full six weeks before anyone wants to hear sleigh bells, and where a handful of artists play the biggest rooms of their careers with no fanfare attached. Read the month in four weekly blocks and the shape becomes obvious.

## Week 1: November 2-8

The month opens on a finale. Fuerza Regida close the second leg of their This Is Our Dream Tour [at Red Rocks on November 2](/en/concert/en-fuerza-regida-this-is-our-dream-morrison-20261102), capping a ten-date run that started in Tinley Park a month earlier — a small room for a group that filled Dodger Stadium and Citi Field earlier in the year.

Then the scale flips. Usher and Chris Brown open [a five-night stand at Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta on November 5](/en/concert/en-usher-chris-brown-rb-tour-atlanta-20261105), continuing on the 6th, 8th, 10th and 11th. The format is worth knowing before you buy: the two do not play back-to-back headline sets but alternate across nine staged acts, with Mario, Eric Bellinger and Tank onstage throughout.

The same night, [Pentatonix](/en/artist/Pentatonix) start the holiday season absurdly early at the [Tacoma Dome](/en/venue/Tacoma%20Dome%2C%20Tacoma%2C%20WA), the first of 28 North American arena dates. A day later [Quadeca reaches Seattle's Showbox](/en/concert/en-quadeca-life-1-tour-seattle-20261106) on a 23-date run behind Life 1, with a portion of proceeds going to City of Hope.

November 7 is the week's collision: Jimmy Eat World and The Format share a stage for the first time [at the Hollywood Bowl](/en/concert/en-jimmy-eat-world-the-format-hollywood-bowl-20261107), playing Bleed American and Dog Problems in full for their 25th and 20th anniversaries, while [Olivia Rodrigo takes two nights in Philadelphia](/en/concert/en-olivia-rodrigo-unraveled-tour-philadelphia-20261107) on a tour now running to 86 shows.

## Week 2: November 9-15

[Stand Up for Heroes turns twenty on November 9](/en/concert/en-stand-up-for-heroes-2026-new-york-20261109) at David Geffen Hall, with Bruce Springsteen headlining a benefit he has played almost every year since 2007 and Jon Stewart, Hasan Minhaj and Josh Johnson handling the comedy. The series has raised more than 117 million dollars.

Midweek belongs to rooms artists have been working toward. [Steve Lacy](/en/artist/Steve%20Lacy) plays [Red Rocks on November 11](/en/concert/en-steve-lacy-oh-yeah-morrison-20261111); the same night [Allison Russell](/en/artist/Allison%20Russell) is at [The Showbox in Seattle](/en/concert/en-allison-russell-hour-of-chaos-seattle-20261111), four days from closing her tour. [Dave Matthews Band](/en/artist/Dave%20Matthews%20Band) finish a six-city fall run with [two nights at Madison Square Garden](/en/concert/en-dave-matthews-band-fall-tour-new-york-20261112).

The weekend is Los Angeles's. [Camp Flog Gnaw returns to the Dodger Stadium lot on November 14 and 15](/en/concert/en-camp-flog-gnaw-carnival-2026-20261114) with Tyler, the Creator, Turnstile, Steve Lacy, Future and Don Toliver — passes sold out weeks ago and only a waitlist remains. [Jorja Smith](/en/artist/Jorja%20Smith) opens her five-date North American run [at the Orpheum the same day](/en/concert/en-jorja-smith-what-are-the-odds-los-angeles-20261114).

## Week 3: November 16-22

The busiest stretch of November 2026. [Oso Oso hit the Teragram Ballroom](/en/concert/en-oso-oso-exchange-of-information-los-angeles-20261115) with Liquid Mike and Bugsy; [Rush](/en/artist/Rush) play [Pittsburgh on the 15th](/en/concert/en-rush-fifty-something-pittsburgh-20261115), one of four cities added late to the Fifty Something Tour. Silverstein and Story of the Year open [Camp Screamo 2 in Seattle](/en/concert/en-silverstein-story-of-the-year-camp-screamo-2-seattle-20261116) on the 16th, the same evening Usher and Chris Brown add [a fourth SoFi Stadium date](/en/concert/en-usher-chris-brown-rb-tour-los-angeles-20261116).

November 18 splits three ways: the Jonas Brothers bring their 2008 tour rebuild [to the Hollywood Bowl](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-hollywood-bowl-20261118) with All Time Low added for this stop only, [Lindsey Stirling launches a 25-city Snow Waltz run](/en/concert/en-lindsey-stirling-snow-waltz-hollywood-20261118) in Florida, and the following night [Jamila Woods](/en/artist/Jamila%20Woods) marks a decade of HEAVN [in Portland](/en/concert/en-jamila-woods-10-years-of-heavn-portland-20261119) while [KATSEYE](/en/artist/KATSEYE) play [Oakland Arena](/en/concert/en-katseye-wildworld-tour-oakland-20261119).

Two days later [Down release Volume V](/en/concert/en-down-volume-v-20261120), their first album in twelve years, led by a single pairing Phil Anselmo with King Diamond. Then November 21 stacks three hometown nights at once: [Steve Lacy's first Hollywood Bowl headline](/en/concert/en-steve-lacy-hollywood-bowl-20261121), [Jorja Smith at Kings Theatre](/en/concert/en-jorja-smith-kings-theatre-20261121), and [KATSEYE at Crypto.com Arena](/en/concert/en-katseye-wildworld-tour-los-angeles-20261121) for the largest Los Angeles shows they have played.

## Week 4: November 23-30

The month thins out and then closes hard. [Nia Archives brings the Emotional World Tour to Alexandra Palace on November 26](/en/concert/en-nia-archives-emotional-world-tour-london-20261126), the London stop of a 19-date run behind Emotional Junglist. Over Thanksgiving weekend, [Rock The Bells returns to Newark's Prudential Center on the 29th](/en/concert/en-rock-the-bells-festival-2026-20261129) to close Hip-Hop History Month, with LL Cool J headlining alongside SWV, Method Man & Redman and Lil' Kim.

What the month rewards is timing. Several of these — Lacy at the Bowl, KATSEYE at Crypto.com, Jorja Smith's theater run — are career-first rooms that will not be this size again on the next tour. The full calendar is on [the WhenStage home page](/en), and every artist above has [their own page](/en/artist) with the rest of their dates.
