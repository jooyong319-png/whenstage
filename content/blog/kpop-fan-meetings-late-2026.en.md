If you follow Korean acts from outside Korea, the calendar for the next six weeks can look oddly crowded with things that are not quite concerts. Between mid-November and early December, [Billlie](/en/artist/Billlie), [CHENLE](/en/artist/CHENLE), [aespa](/en/artist/aespa), [Gong Yoo](/en/artist/Gong%20Yoo) and [AND2BLE](/en/artist/AND2BLE) all hold a fan meeting or fan concert in Seoul. None of them is a tour stop in the usual sense, and buying a ticket to one works differently from buying a ticket to an arena show. Here is what the format actually is, why so many land at the end of the year, and the rules that trip up first-time buyers.

## What you are buying

A fan meeting is built around the relationship between an artist and their fandom rather than around a setlist. Songs are usually part of it, but so are talk segments, games and scripted bits. [Hwang Minhyun's PEACH-BLOSSOM](/en/concert/en-hwang-minhyun-peach-blossom-seoul-20261016), which opened with three days at Woori Bank Hall in COEX Artium in October, is a clean example: the programme mixed vocal performances with talk and game segments.

The "fan con" sits between the two formats. [HIGHLIGHT's fan con at Jangchung Arena](/en/concert/en-highlight-fan-con-seoul-20261016) in October ran on an isekai storyline that cast the 18-year veterans as rookie trainees, and [10CM's fan concert THE MISSING TRACKS](/en/concert/en-10cm-the-missing-tracks-seoul-20261009) went hunting for songs that had been dropped from past set lists or never played live, with a different case-number subtitle each night. The common thread is a concept written for people who already know the catalogue. If you are a casual listener, a regular tour date will usually serve you better than a fan meeting; if you are deep in, these are often the more memorable shows.

## Why they cluster at the end of the year

Look at what each event is attached to and a pattern appears: anniversaries and birthdays. [Billlie's The Heist Squad](/en/concert/en-billlie-the-heist-squad-fancon-seoul-20261115) on November 15 marks five years since their debut, a single roughly 100-minute show at Sogang University's Mary Hall Grand Theater. [CHENLE's Lele's Show 2](/en/concert/en-chenle-leles-show-2-seoul-20261124) on November 24 is the NCT DREAM member's birthday party at YES24 Live Hall, a year after the first edition. [aespa's fan meeting at Korea University](/en/concert/en-aespa-my-bogosipda-ae-fanmeeting-seoul-20261128) on November 28 and 29 is timed to their sixth debut anniversary.

Then there are first times. [AND2BLE's W!nter inside](/en/concert/en-and2ble-winter-inside-fancon-seoul-20261205) on December 5 and 6 is the group's first fan-con, and it travels to Yokohama on December 11 and 12. These events are where a fandom marks its own calendar, which is why they are so dense in the last stretch of the year.

## Actors do this too

The fan meeting is not only a K-pop format. [Gong Yoo's The Long Take](/en/concert/en-gong-yoo-the-long-take-seoul-20261128) is his first-ever Asia fan meeting tour: six cities starting in Yokohama on October 4, through Bangkok, Jakarta, Macau and Manila, finishing at Yonsei University's Grand Auditorium in Seoul on November 28. [Kim Ji-won's WONEDERLAND](/en/concert/en-kim-ji-won-wonederland-seoul-20261024), her first solo fan event in two years, opened in Seoul in October before an Asia run that includes Macau, Taipei, Yokohama, Osaka, Hong Kong and Bangkok. Hwang Minhyun's tour likewise moves on to Macau on November 7 and Taipei on November 27. For fans outside Korea, these Asia legs are often the realistic way in.

## The ticket rules are stricter than you expect

This is where fan meetings differ most from Western concert tickets. Almost every event in this list sells through a single Korean platform, uses a fan club presale before general sale, and caps purchases hard.

- **Per-person limits.** aespa allows one ticket per person per show; CHENLE one per person; Billlie two per person.
- **Name matching.** For Lele's Show 2, the booking name, the fan club member name and the attendee name must all match, and resale tickets may be refused at the door.
- **Passport verification for overseas buyers.** aespa's Melon Ticket Global page only lets passport-verified accounts purchase, and AND2BLE's overseas buyers must finish passport verification on Melon's global page before buying.
- **Flat pricing.** Instead of tiered seating, many of these use one price for every seat: 121,000 won for Billlie and aespa, 143,000 won for AND2BLE, 77,000 won for CHENLE.

Most of these sales have already opened, but the same system applies to regular concerts. [N.Flying's Christmas shows, Once Upon a Snowball](/en/concert/en-n-flying-once-upon-a-snowball-seoul-20261224), require fan club verification from October 30, a member sale on November 4 and general sale on November 6, and fans who verify on Melon's global page must also book there. If you are planning a December trip, that is the next one to watch.

## If you cannot travel

Some events build in a way to watch from home. The final day of [DAY6's fifth fan meeting at Inspire Arena](/en/concert/en-day6-5th-fanmeeting-incheon-20261023) was streamed worldwide as a paid Beyond LIVE broadcast, and [MONSTA X's encore nights in Incheon](/en/concert/en-monsta-x-the-x-nexus-encore-incheon-20261121) on November 21 and 22 are carried as a paid online stream. Not every fan meeting offers a stream, so check the individual listing before assuming one will exist.
