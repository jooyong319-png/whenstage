The first two months of the year are usually where touring calendars go quiet. The holiday finales are over, spring tours have not started, and most artists are in a studio or on a beach. That makes the winter shows that do get booked more interesting than their numbers suggest: a date in late January is rarely an accident. It is a reunion that needed a fixed point, a homecoming timed for the off-season, or a band choosing the one room that means something to it.

WhenStage currently lists a little over twenty concerts, festivals and releases for January and February 2027. Below are the eight we think matter most, ranked by a mix of how long fans have waited, how much the room changes the show, and how hard the tickets have been to get. Everything here is already on sale or already announced, so this is a planning list, not a wish list.

## 1. System of a Down & Faith No More — Australia & New Zealand

The top spot was not close. [System of a Down and Faith No More's four-stadium run](/en/concert/en-system-of-a-down-faith-no-more-sydney-20270122) opens at Accor Stadium in Sydney on January 22, then moves to Marvel Stadium in Melbourne (January 27), Suncorp Stadium in Brisbane (February 1) and Hnry Stadium in Wellington (February 7). It is Faith No More's first concerts in nearly a decade and System of a Down's first Australian dates since 2012. Either fact alone would justify a high ranking. Together, on a double bill, in stadiums, it is the defining heavy show of the southern summer and of our northern winter.

## 2. Ride — O2 Academy Brixton, London

Shoegaze reunions are common now; reunions with a built-in story are not. [Ride's Here And Now tour](/en/concert/en-ride-here-and-now-london-20270213) is six UK dates and the band's first live shows in three years, opening in Bournemouth on February 12 and closing in Glasgow on February 19. The London night on February 13 sends Andy Bell, Mark Gardener, Steve Queralt and Laurence Colbert back to Brixton 34 years after the 1992 concert released as Ride - Live at Brixton Academy. For longtime fans, that is the date to circle.

## 3. Epik High — KSPO Dome, Seoul

Two decades into their career, [Epik High headline KSPO Dome for the first time](/en/concert/en-epik-high-2027-concert-seoul-20270122), across three shows from January 22 to 24. It arrives after the trio's 13-city EPIK HIGH 3.0 tour of North America, which makes this feel less like a routine winter concert and more like a victory lap at home. The general sale opened October 7 on Melon Ticket, so this is one to check today rather than in January.

## 4. Billy Strings — Bridgestone Arena and the Ryman, Nashville

[Billy Strings' Winter Tour 2027](/en/concert/en-billy-strings-winter-2027-nashville-20270219) is only ten shows, opening with five nights in Asheville and ending in his adopted hometown. The structure is the clever part: two arena nights at Bridgestone starting February 19, then [a closing show at the Ryman Auditorium](/en/concert/en-billy-strings-winter-2027-ryman-20270221) on February 21 that only Bridgestone ticket buyers could enter the draw for. An arena-sized bluegrass player stepping down into the room where he made his debut in 2018 is the most Nashville thing on this list.

## 5. NCT WISH — Gocheok Sky Dome, Seoul

[NCT WISH open their second concert tour with two nights at Gocheok Sky Dome](/en/concert/en-nct-wish-into-the-wish-face-our-wish-seoul-20270115) on January 15 and 16, the group's first time headlining the domed ballpark less than three years after debut. That is a fast climb by any standard. Seoul comes before stops in Kaohsiung, Bangkok and Manila, and Melon Ticket runs a separate global page that requires passport verification before you can buy.

## 6. John Mulaney — Mulaney Takes Manhattan

The one non-music entry earns its place by format alone. [Mulaney Takes Manhattan](/en/concert/en-john-mulaney-takes-manhattan-msg-20270115) is nine nights in nine New York rooms, starting January 7 at the Comedy Cellar and climbing through the Blue Note, Joe's Pub, Bowery Ballroom, the Music Box Theatre, the Beacon Theatre, Carnegie Hall and Radio City before finishing at Madison Square Garden on January 15. It is a tour of the city's venues as much as a comedy run.

## 7. Basement — Roundhouse, London

[Basement's WIRED tour](/en/concert/en-basement-wired-tour-london-20270218) supports the band's first album in more than eight years, and the Roundhouse on February 18 is the biggest London date of a 15-show UK and European run. Fleshwater and Beton Arme open every night. After that long a gap, the size of the London room says a lot about how much the audience waited.

## 8. Fujii Kaze — Gocheok Sky Dome, Seoul

[Fujii Kaze's Prema World Tour stop in Seoul](/en/concert/en-fujii-kaze-prema-world-tour-seoul-20270109) on January 9 is his largest Korean show to date, in the country's only domed stadium. A Japanese singer-songwriter filling the same building as K-pop's biggest acts is a useful marker of how far his audience has traveled, and it opens the winter on a high note.

## Also worth your winter

The Eagles add four more [Sphere dates in Las Vegas](/en/concert/en-eagles-sphere-residency-las-vegas-20270115) in January, extending a run that now stands at seventy-two shows. 70000 Tons of Metal sails from Miami on January 14 with roughly sixty bands and only 3,000 tickets. David Byrne's Night, a tribute collective pairing Talking Heads songs with Robert Burns poetry, plays Manchester and then its biggest-ever show at Troxy in London. Simple Minds release Sacrosanct on January 29, Dinosaur Jr. start a new leg at Brooklyn Steel on February 11, and Jake Bugg ends a solo tour behind Alamo at Union Chapel on February 26.

None of these winter dates are filler. For the full list, with ticket notes, see the [calendar](/en).
