January is usually where the live calendar goes quiet: holiday tours are over, spring routing has not started, and most bands are home. The January 2027 listings on WhenStage do not look like that at all. They look like a month that belongs to two very different groups of people. On one side are the acts finishing something that began in 2026, mostly residencies that never left one room for long. On the other is Seoul and the airport arena next door, where the first weeks of the year have become the moment artists from Korea and Japan choose for their biggest local shows.

Read the month week by week and that split is easy to see.

## Week 1: January 1-10

The year opens mid-residency. [Lord Huron](/en/artist/Lord%20Huron) pick 23 Nights at the Broken Bottle back up on New Year's Day at [Channel 24 in Sacramento](/en/concert/en-lord-huron-broken-bottle-sacramento-20270101), the 15th of the 23 dates and the first show after a 12-day break that follows Brooklyn. Las Vegas comes on the 2nd and Oakland's Fox Theater on the 3rd, so the first weekend of January 2027 is effectively a West Coast sprint for the band.

On the 2nd and 3rd, [HANRORO](/en/artist/HANRORO) plays her first arena shows. Question Mark, her fifth solo concert, runs at [Inspire Arena near Incheon Airport](/en/concert/en-hanroro-question-mark-incheon-20270102) with a standing floor and reserved seats on the upper levels. For a singer-songwriter whose guitar-driven songs about youthful restlessness began with a 2022 debut single, it is a fast climb, and it sets the tone for the room: Inspire Arena hosts a second headliner, for three more nights, before the month is half over.

The 9th is the busiest single night of the opening week. Fujii Kaze brings the Prema World Tour to [Gocheok Sky Dome](/en/concert/en-fujii-kaze-prema-world-tour-seoul-20270109), his largest Korean show to date and the country's only domed stadium. Across town, rapper Simon Dominic finally headlines his first solo concert, [SIMON SAYS at YES24 Live Hall](/en/concert/en-simon-dominic-simon-says-seoul-20270109), across the 9th and 10th, while EXO-KASE, the new unit of Kai and Sehun, open their [SILENT CITY fan-concert tour](/en/concert/en-exo-kase-silent-city-fancon-seoul-20270109) at Korea University's Hwajung Gymnasium. Overseas fans should note the dates on that last one: the global presale for EXO-L members is November 24 and general sale November 25, both at 8 p.m. KST.

## Week 2: January 11-17

The residency closes at home. Lord Huron finish the Broken Bottle run with two nights at The Wiltern, and [the final show on the 11th](/en/concert/en-lord-huron-broken-bottle-los-angeles-20270111) ends a 23-night project on home turf in their home city.

Midweek, two very different ideas of a destination show begin. [70000 Tons of Metal](/en/concert/en-70000-tons-of-metal-2027-20270114) sails from Miami to Puerto Plata and back between January 14 and 18 aboard Royal Caribbean's Freedom of the Seas, with roughly sixty bands and only 3,000 tickets. The same evening, [Kenshi Yonezu](/en/artist/Kenshi%20Yonezu) opens three nights of the GHOST world tour at [Inspire Arena](/en/concert/en-kenshi-yonezu-ghost-world-tour-incheon-20270114), one more show than his sold-out 2025 Korean debut in the same building, and the first overseas stop of the tour's 2027 leg.

Friday the 15th is crowded in three countries. The [Eagles](/en/artist/Eagles) return to [Sphere in Las Vegas](/en/concert/en-eagles-sphere-residency-las-vegas-20270115) for the first of four added 2027 dates, taking their run to seventy-two shows. John Mulaney ends his nine-room New York residency [at Madison Square Garden](/en/concert/en-john-mulaney-takes-manhattan-msg-20270115), after starting at the Comedy Cellar on the 7th and working up through Carnegie Hall and Radio City. In Manchester, David Byrne's Night opens its UK tour at [New Century Hall](/en/concert/en-david-byrnes-night-manchester-20270115), pairing Talking Heads songs with Robert Burns poetry. And in Seoul, [NCT WISH headline Gocheok Sky Dome](/en/concert/en-nct-wish-into-the-wish-face-our-wish-seoul-20270115) for the first time, on the 15th and 16th.

The weekend keeps going. aespa play [The O2 in London on the 16th](/en/concert/en-aespa-synk-complaexity-london-20270116), two days after Manchester, with a production billed as a full rebuild rather than a revival. [HYUKOH](/en/artist/HYUKOH) open their first solo world tour in about five years at [Olympic Hall](/en/concert/en-hyukoh-33-tour-seoul-20270116) ahead of the album '33', and trot star Young Tak closes TAK SHOW5 [at BEXCO in Busan](/en/concert/en-young-tak-tak-show5-busan-20270116) on the 16th and 17th.

## Week 3: January 18-24

This is the week of first times. [EPIK HIGH](/en/artist/EPIK%20HIGH) headline [KSPO Dome](/en/concert/en-epik-high-2027-concert-seoul-20270122) for the first time in two decades, with three shows from the 22nd to the 24th following a 13-city North American tour. On the 22nd, System of a Down and Faith No More open their stadium run [at Accor Stadium in Sydney](/en/concert/en-system-of-a-down-faith-no-more-sydney-20270122), Faith No More's first concerts in nearly a decade.

On the 23rd, David Byrne's Night plays [Troxy in east London](/en/concert/en-david-byrnes-night-london-20270123), the largest headline show the collective has staged, with players drawn from English Teacher, The Pogues, The Horrors and others. In Seoul, rock band TOUCHED bring the fifth edition of their winter brand show, [HIGHLIGHT V](/en/concert/en-touched-highlight-v-seoul-20270123), to Hwajung Gymnasium, while SUPER JUNIOR-83z add a [Busan stop to their 1983 fan-concert tour](/en/concert/en-super-junior-83z-1983-fancon-busan-20270123) at KBS Busan Hall.

## Week 4: January 25-31

The month thins out, but it ends with two returns. Maroon 5 play [KINTEX in Goyang on the 27th](/en/concert/en-maroon-5-asia-2027-seoul-20270127), their first headline date in Korea in roughly three years, the same night System of a Down and Faith No More reach Melbourne. Then on the 29th, [Simple Minds](/en/artist/Simple%20Minds) release [Sacrosanct](/en/concert/en-simple-minds-sacrosanct-20270129), an eight-track studio album through BMG that follows 2022's Direction Of The Heart.

## How to plan around it

If you follow North American or UK acts, January 2027 is mostly about catching the end of something: a residency's last night, a comedy run's arena finale, a tribute tour's biggest room. If you follow Korean or Japanese artists, it is the opposite, a month of openers and debuts in bigger buildings than these acts have played before. Most of those Seoul tickets went on sale in October and November, and EXO-KASE's global sale is still days away, so the practical move is to check each event page now rather than waiting for the new year.
