Look at the Australian and New Zealand dates on the calendar between now and the middle of 2027 and the first thing you notice is not the names. It is how few buildings are involved. Four separate international runs, spread across eight months, and almost every date lands in one of about six rooms — [RAC Arena](/en/venue/RAC%20Arena%2C%20Perth) in Perth, [Rod Laver Arena](/en/venue/Rod%20Laver%20Arena%2C%20Melbourne) in Melbourne, [Afterpay Arena](/en/venue/Afterpay%20Arena%2C%20Sydney) in Sydney, and the stadiums in Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane.

That is the shape of touring Australia. The distances are long, the markets are few, and the same promoters book the same venues over and over. What changes is how each act chooses to use them.

## October: Tame Impala come home

The first of the four is already here. [Tame Impala](/en/artist/Tame%20Impala) bring the Deadbeat Tour back to Australia in [October 2026](/en/concert/en-tame-impala-deadbeat-perth-20261024), opening with two nights at RAC Arena in Perth on October 24 and 25 — Kevin Parker's hometown, and the natural place to close out a year-long run behind the band's fifth album.

The scale here is worth noting, because it is the exception rather than the rule. The Frontier Touring run also takes in Brisbane, **four nights** at Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne and **three** in Sydney. Ninajirachi supports every date, and the band plays as a six-piece on a stage set in the round. Nine-plus arena nights for one act is a volume of tickets that very few international artists can move in Australia; Tame Impala can because this is, in effect, a home tour.

## January: the stadium exception

Then the pattern breaks. [System of a Down and Faith No More](/en/concert/en-system-of-a-down-faith-no-more-sydney-20270122) do not play arenas at all. They play four stadiums, one per market, and then leave: Accor Stadium in Sydney on January 22, [Marvel Stadium](/en/venue/Marvel%20Stadium%2C%20Melbourne) in Melbourne on January 27, [Suncorp Stadium](/en/venue/Suncorp%20Stadium%2C%20Brisbane) in Brisbane on February 1, and Hnry Stadium in Wellington on February 7.

That is the co-headline logic. Neither band has been through recently — these are Faith No More's first concerts in nearly a decade, and System of a Down's first Australian dates since 2012 — so instead of stacking arena nights the way a touring act with a current album would, they put two long absences on one bill and take the single biggest room in each city. One night, maximum capacity, done. An artist mailing-list presale opened on July 28 with general tickets following on July 31, staggered market by market.

It is the same building Oasis used on their Live '25 run, when two sold-out nights at Accor Stadium drew roughly 140,000 people between them. Australia has a stadium tier; it just gets used sparingly.

## February: the arena circuit, by the book

[Bryan Adams](/en/artist/Bryan%20Adams) runs the textbook version. The Roll With The Punches Tour opens February 23 at RAC Arena in Perth and closes March 12 at [Wolfbrook Arena](/en/venue/Wolfbrook%20Arena%2C%20Christchurch) in Christchurch — a clean Frontier Touring arena loop behind his sixteenth studio album, with Natalie Imbruglia as special guest.

One detail in that run says more about demand in Australia than any chart position would: the Melbourne date at Rod Laver Arena sold out, and a second show was added. That is the normal mechanism here. Acts book one night per city, see what happens, and add rather than guess high.

## May: Radiohead book residencies instead

And then the most unusual approach of the four. [Radiohead](/en/artist/Radiohead) return to Australia in May 2027 for their first tour of the country in fifteen years, and they do not tour it so much as settle into it. Four nights at RAC Arena in Perth from [May 3](/en/concert/en-radiohead-australia-perth-20270503). Six at Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne from [May 13](/en/concert/en-radiohead-australia-melbourne-20270513). Six more at Afterpay Arena in Sydney from [May 25](/en/concert/en-radiohead-australia-sydney-20270525). Then residencies in Japan.

Sixteen arena nights in three cities is a completely different model from the four stadium nights in January, and it follows the band's European residency shows of late 2025. The ticketing matched the format: sales ran only through a registration ballot on the band's own site, capped at four per fan, specifically to keep bots and resellers out. No general on-sale rush, no staggered Ticketmaster windows.

## What the rooms tell you

Put the four together and the venue list does the explaining. The same Perth arena opens three of these runs. Rod Laver Arena appears in three of them, and Afterpay Arena — which only took that name in July 2026, after being rebranded from Qudos Bank Arena, with [Laufey](/en/artist/Laufey)'s two-night August stand among its first major concerts under the new sign — takes Radiohead's longest stretch.

So when something unusual shows up in Australia, it is rarely a new building. It is an act deciding how many nights to take in an old one: one stadium show if you have been away too long to risk more, nine arena nights if the country is home, sixteen across three cities if you would rather run a ballot than a tour.
