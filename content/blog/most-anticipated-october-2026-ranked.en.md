September has barely started and October is already the more crowded month. Between October 1 and October 31, three separate stadium-scale runs pass through North America, an Australian arena tour comes home after a year abroad, and a band that has not played live since 2024 resurfaces for exactly one show in the Basque Country. Ranking any of this is subjective, but the criteria here are consistent: how large the rooms are, how hard the tickets were to get, and how long people have been waiting. Here are the eight nights we would clear the calendar for.

## 1. Usher and Chris Brown, The R&B Tour

Nothing else this month operates at this size. The co-headline run sold out its opening stretch so quickly that eight stadium dates were added in August, pushing the Live Nation trek to 59 shows total. [MetLife Stadium on October 15](/en/concert/en-usher-chris-brown-rb-tour-east-rutherford-20261015) is the marquee one, with a Halloween-night stop at [Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis](/en/concert/en-usher-chris-brown-rb-tour-indianapolis-20261031) closing the month. The format is the interesting part: rather than two headline sets, the singers alternate across nine cinematic acts over nearly three hours, with Mario, Eric Bellinger and Tank onstage throughout. Earlier legs set attendance records in Detroit, Washington and Birmingham.

## 2. JAY-Z, 30th Anniversary

[JAY-Z](/en/artist/JAY-Z) plays [SoFi Stadium](/en/venue/SoFi%20Stadium%2C%20Los%20Angeles%2C%20CA) across two October nights, [the 23rd and 24th](/en/concert/en-jay-z-30th-anniversary-los-angeles-20261023), marking three decades since Reasonable Doubt. The Los Angeles dates were announced in late July alongside Tottenham Hotspur Stadium and Stade de France, and they follow three hometown nights at Yankee Stadium in July. Anniversary shows can be lazy; a thirty-year catalogue at stadium scale is not.

## 3. Jonas Brothers, The Burning Up Tour All Over Again

Forty-five North American arena stops, built out of the sold-out Madison Square Garden celebrations in August. The premise is unusually specific — a rebuild of the 2008 tour that broke the band nationally, staged for rooms roughly ten times the size. [Philadelphia on October 2](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-burning-up-all-over-again-philadelphia-20261002) opens the stretch, with Indianapolis and Rosemont following mid-month.

## 4. Tame Impala, Deadbeat Tour

Kevin Parker brings the year-long Deadbeat run home, with two October nights at Perth's [RAC Arena](/en/venue/RAC%20Arena%2C%20Perth) from [the 24th](/en/concert/en-tame-impala-deadbeat-perth-20261024), plus four at Rod Laver and three in Sydney. The band plays as a six-piece on a stage set in the round, with Ninajirachi supporting every date.

## 5. TAEMIN, LiMiNaL World Tour

[TAEMIN](/en/artist/TAEMIN) takes the North American leg into [Crypto.com Arena](/en/venue/Crypto.com%20Arena%2C%20Los%20Angeles%2C%20CA) on [October 15](/en/concert/en-taemin-liminal-crypto-com-arena-20261015). It is a compact six-city run — San Jose on October 12 through Newark on October 23 — which makes each date harder to catch than the length of the tour suggests.

## 6. Steve Lacy, Oh yeah? Tour

[Steve Lacy](/en/artist/Steve%20Lacy) makes his [Radio City Music Hall](/en/venue/Radio%20City%20Music%20Hall%2C%20New%20York%2C%20NY) debut on [October 10](/en/concert/en-steve-lacy-radio-city-20261010), on his first headline run since 2022. The 27-city outing supports his third album, released in July with SZA, Erykah Badu and Tyler, the Creator among the guests.

## 7. Tyla, A*POP World Tour

[Tyla](/en/artist/Tyla) reaches [O2 Academy Brixton](/en/venue/O2%20Academy%20Brixton%2C%20London) on the same October evening, [the 15th](/en/concert/en-tyla-apop-world-tour-london-20261015), five nights into a 34-date run behind her second album. The European leg runs first; North America waits until November.

## 8. The National at AB100

[The National](/en/artist/The%20National) close a two-day gathering curated by Aaron and Bryce Dessner at [Kursaal Donostia](/en/venue/Kursaal%20Donostia%2C%20San%20Sebastian%2C%20Spain) on [the 18th](/en/concert/en-the-national-ab100-san-sebastian-20261018) — their only confirmed concert of the year, and their first performance since the end of 2024. Kevin Morby, Ben Howard and Jon Hopkins are among the collaborators.

## Also worth your month

[Ringo Starr](/en/artist/Ringo%20Starr%20%26%20His%20All%20Starr%20Band) and His All Starr Band play Forest Hills Stadium on October 1, near the end of a ten-date trek. [Phish](/en/artist/Phish) open an eight-show run with three consecutive nights in Atlantic City, including a Jacksonville date that is their first in Florida since 2016. And [Monsta X](/en/artist/Monsta%20X) bring THE X : NEXUS to Madison Square Garden's Infosys Theater on the 6th, part of a ten-city leg running through the 24th.

The full October calendar is on the [site's front page](/en), and the [artist index](/en/artist) will tell you whether any of these acts come back around later in the year.
