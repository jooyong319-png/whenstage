Most cities have a concert season. Los Angeles has something closer to a rotation. There is no single festival weekend that defines the fall here and no one arena that everything routes through — instead, between the end of September 2026 and the second week of January 2027, roughly a dozen significant shows land, and the fastest way to make sense of them is not by genre or by month but by the room each one chose.

The choice matters more than it sounds. A stadium anniversary show and a two-night theater residency are not the same event scaled up or down; they are different bets about what an audience wants. Here is how the season sorts itself out, venue by venue.

## The Hollywood Bowl gets four nights that agree on nothing

The [Hollywood Bowl](/en/venue/Hollywood%20Bowl%2C%20Los%20Angeles%2C%20CA) is the closest thing the city has to a shared stage, and this fall it hosts four bookings that could not be less alike.

It starts on September 29, when Erykah Badu and producer The Alchemist [close their LIVE: A September Tour](/en/concert/en-erykah-badu-alchemist-hollywood-bowl-20260929) there. The short run is the first extended live outing behind the duo's collaborative album, released August 28 through Control FREAQ and Young Recordings. De La Soul open most of the tour; for the Los Angeles finale, Smino takes the slot instead — a small swap, but the kind that tells you the last night was planned as its own thing rather than a copy of the previous twelve.

Six weeks later, on November 7, the Bowl turns into a nostalgia machine of a very specific vintage: Jimmy Eat World and The Format [co-headline for the first time ever](/en/concert/en-jimmy-eat-world-the-format-hollywood-bowl-20261107), each playing an album straight through. Jimmy Eat World do *Bleed American* in full for its twenty-fifth anniversary — Sweetness, The Middle, Hear You Me — and The Format answer with 2006's *Dog Problems* for its twentieth. Both bands come out of Arizona, they have never shared a stage before, and it is one of only two full-album shows The Format have on the books this year.

Then November 18 brings the [Jonas Brothers](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-hollywood-bowl-20261118), who are running an idea even more literal than an anniversary: The Burning Up Tour All Over Again rebuilds the set list and staging of their 2008-09 tour across 47 North American dates, with later catalogue hits folded in. The Los Angeles date is one of the few stops with a third support act — All Time Low join Magnus Ferrell and Deleasa here only.

And three nights after that, on November 21, [Steve Lacy](/en/artist/Steve%20Lacy) [headlines the Bowl for the first time](/en/concert/en-steve-lacy-hollywood-bowl-20261121). It is one of three milestone venue debuts on his Oh yeah? Tour, alongside Radio City Music Hall and Red Rocks. So within fifteen days the same amphitheater hosts a band replaying a 2008 tour and a Compton-raised guitarist playing the room for the first time in his life.

## The stadiums: two weekends, two different theories of a big show

Only two stadium events land in the city this fall, and they are almost opposites.

On October 23 and 24, [JAY-Z](/en/artist/JAY-Z) plays [SoFi Stadium](/en/concert/en-jay-z-30th-anniversary-los-angeles-20261023) as part of the run marking thirty years since *Reasonable Doubt*. The Los Angeles dates were announced July 27 alongside stadium shows at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in London and Stade de France in Paris, and they follow three hometown nights at Yankee Stadium in July. This is the catalog-as-monument model: one artist, three decades, the biggest room available.

Three weeks later, on November 14 and 15, [Camp Flog Gnaw Carnival](/en/concert/en-camp-flog-gnaw-carnival-2026-20261114) returns to the [Dodger Stadium](/en/venue/Dodger%20Stadium%2C%20Los%20Angeles%2C%20CA) lot with the opposite proposition — no single headliner carrying the weight, but a deep bill under Tyler, the Creator's name. Turnstile, Steve Lacy, Future and Don Toliver headline alongside him, with Vince Staples, Earl Sweatshirt, J.I.D, Lupe Fiasco, Isaiah Rashad, Wallows, Magdalena Bay, Lola Young and Syd also billed. Goldenvoice announced the lineup as a set of word scrambles, and passes sold out weeks ago; only a waitlist remains. Note the overlap, too: Lacy plays the Dodger Stadium lot on the 14th and comes back to the Hollywood Bowl on the 21st.

## One arena night

[Crypto.com Arena](/en/venue/Crypto.com%20Arena%2C%20Los%20Angeles%2C%20CA) gets a single date worth circling. On October 15, [TAEMIN](/en/artist/TAEMIN) brings the [LiMiNaL World Tour](/en/concert/en-taemin-liminal-crypto-com-arena-20261015) there. The SHINee member's North American leg is short — six cities, opening October 12 at SAP Center in San Jose and finishing October 23 at Prudential Center in Newark, with Las Vegas, Grand Prairie and Chicago in between. AEG is promoting the United States dates. Six-city runs do not leave much room for a second chance, which is generally what makes them sell.

## The theaters are where the season actually lives

If the stadiums are the headlines, the theater bookings are the more interesting reading.

[Jorja Smith](/en/artist/Jorja%20Smith) opens her five-date North American run at the [Orpheum Theatre](/en/concert/en-jorja-smith-what-are-the-odds-los-angeles-20261114) on November 14 — the same night Camp Flog Gnaw starts across town. The run follows the August 21 release of her album *What Are The Odds* on FAMM and caps a heavy year that included three nights supporting Harry Styles in Mexico City plus festival slots across Europe and South America. Five dates total: Los Angeles, San Francisco, Toronto, Brooklyn and Chicago.

On December 2, [BEAT](/en/concert/en-beat-fall-tour-inglewood-20261202) reaches YouTube Theater in Inglewood for the penultimate night of a 40-date tour that ends the following day in Riverside. The band pairs former King Crimson members Adrian Belew and Tony Levin with Steve Vai and Terry Bozzio, the latter standing in for Danny Carey because of Tool commitments, and the repertoire has widened past the 1980s Crimson albums into the band's 1990s material.

The season then closes past the new year. On January 11, 2027, [Lord Huron](/en/artist/Lord%20Huron) [finish their 23-night residency](/en/concert/en-lord-huron-broken-bottle-los-angeles-20270111) with the second of two shows at [The Wiltern](/en/venue/The%20Wiltern%2C%20Los%20Angeles%2C%20CA), in their home city. Each night of 23 Nights at the Broken Bottle is staged inside a fictional desert honky-tonk from the band's own invented universe, billed as An Evening With, no opener, drawing on the full catalog from radio hits to rarely played deep cuts. It is a deliberate downshift: after playing amphitheaters through summer 2026 behind *The Cosmic Selector Vol. 1*, they spend the winter in theaters instead.

## And one on the sand

Before any of the above, there is a new entry on the calendar entirely. On September 26 and 27, Goldenvoice — the Coachella and Stagecoach promoter — launches the [Ocean Way Festival](/en/concert/en-ocean-way-festival-2026-20260926) on Santa Monica Beach. The Killers, Jack White and Olivia Dean headline an inaugural bill that also includes Khruangbin, Sublime, Alvvays, Durand Jones & The Indications, Hiatus Kaiyote, SG Lewis, BLOND:ISH, Poolside and a Hot Chip DJ set. Single-day and two-day general admission passes went on sale in July.

## How to plan around it

The compressed stretch is mid-November: Camp Flog Gnaw and Jorja Smith's opening night on the 14th, the Jonas Brothers on the 18th, Steve Lacy on the 21st. If you are budgeting, that week takes the biggest bite. The quieter windows — late September at the Bowl, mid-October at Crypto.com Arena, December in Inglewood — are where the good seats will still exist a month out.
