Los Angeles sorts its season by the room, and so does New York — but the rooms here come in many more sizes, and they sit closer together. Between late November 2026 and May 2027, the city and its immediate orbit host everything from a 25th and final tour date in a Manhattan club to a nine-night comedy residency that climbs from the Comedy Cellar to Madison Square Garden. Genre is a weak guide to any of it. The choice of room says more about what each artist is trying to do.

Here is the next six months, read venue by venue.

## Kings Theatre: Brooklyn's big night out

Brooklyn's [Kings Theatre](/en/venue/Kings%20Theatre%2C%20Brooklyn%2C%20NY) is where touring artists go when they want a New York date that feels like an occasion rather than a stop. Two very different bookings use it that way this winter.

On November 21, [Jorja Smith](/en/concert/en-jorja-smith-kings-theatre-20261121) plays the fourth of only five North American theater dates she announced in early August. The run supports *What Are The Odds*, her August album on FAMM, largely produced by P2J and built on UK garage, grime, two-step and house rather than the soul balladry of her earlier records. Five theaters is a deliberately small footprint, and choosing an ornate room for a dance-leaning record is a statement about how she wants it heard.

A month later, on December 18 and 19, [Lord Huron](/en/concert/en-lord-huron-broken-bottle-brooklyn-20261218) bring the 13th and 14th nights of their 23 Nights at the Broken Bottle residency tour to the same stage. These are the last shows of the December leg and the only New York City stop, arriving straight from two nights at MTELUS in Montreal before a holiday break and the West Coast swing.

## Brooklyn Paramount and Brooklyn Steel: the working rooms

If Kings Theatre is for occasions, the borough's other big rooms are where tours actually do their business. [Syd](/en/artist/Syd) plays [Brooklyn Paramount](/en/concert/en-syd-beard-tour-brooklyn-20261201) on December 1 as her 23-date Beard Tour reaches the Northeast, with Washington, Philadelphia, Boston and Toronto still to follow.

[Brooklyn Steel](/en/venue/Brooklyn%20Steel%2C%20Brooklyn%2C%20NY) gets two openers in 2027, and both say something about the artist. [Dinosaur Jr.](/en/concert/en-dinosaur-jr-there-near-brooklyn-20270211) start the 2027 leg of their There Near tour with the first of two nights there on February 11, with Starcrawler supporting. And on April 2 and 3, [Beirut](/en/concert/en-beirut-north-american-tour-brooklyn-20270402) open Zach Condon's first North American tour since 2019 in the same room — a six-date run behind *A Study of Losses*, the band's seventh album. When an artist who has been away that long starts a tour with a double night in Brooklyn, it is a bet that the audience never left.

## Manhattan's clubs: residencies and finales

In Manhattan's clubs, the scale shrinks and the stakes get more personal. The [Bowery Ballroom](/en/venue/Bowery%20Ballroom%2C%20New%20York%2C%20NY) hosts a long-running New York December ritual: [Yo La Tengo](/en/concert/en-yo-la-tengo-hanukkah-new-york-20261204) play eight consecutive nights of their Hanukkah benefit residency from December 4 to 11. Each night pairs a surprise comedian and a surprise musical guest with a set that repeats no song across the run, usually closing with an all-covers encore, and the proceeds go to charity. It is the opposite of a tour stop: the band stays put and the city comes to them.

The same club takes a very different booking in the spring, when Danish punk band [Iceage](/en/concert/en-iceage-north-american-tour-new-york-20270412) play it on April 12, one of ten shows behind *For Love of Grace & the Hereafter*.

Across Manhattan, [Irving Plaza](/en/concert/en-amorphis-insomnium-new-york-20261219) closes the Amorphis and Insomnium co-headline tour on December 19 — the 25th and final date of a run that began in Boston on November 17, with Texas band Hinayana opening. Tours often end where they can make the last night feel big, and a Manhattan club with a metal crowd is a sensible place to do it.

## The arenas: Long Island and Newark

The biggest rooms in the New York area are not all in the city. The [Jonas Brothers](/en/artist/Jonas%20Brothers) bring The Burning Up Tour All Over Again to [UBS Arena](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-burning-up-all-over-again-belmont-park-20261219) in Belmont Park on December 19, then finish the whole tour two nights later at [Prudential Center](/en/concert/en-jonas-brothers-prudential-center-20261221) in Newark on December 21. The run grew out of sold-out Madison Square Garden celebrations in August 2026 and rebuilds their 2008 Burning Up Tour for arenas.

[Prudential Center](/en/venue/Prudential%20Center%2C%20Newark%2C%20NJ) is quietly the region's most interesting arena calendar. On November 29, [Rock The Bells Festival](/en/concert/en-rock-the-bells-festival-2026-20261129) returns there to close Hip-Hop History Month over Thanksgiving weekend, with founder LL Cool J headlining alongside SWV, Method Man & Redman and Lil' Kim, plus a D-Nice-curated set. And on May 9, 2027, Mexican pop veterans Yuri and Lucero end the US leg of their [Entre Amigas Tour](/en/concert/en-yuri-lucero-entre-amigas-newark-20270509) there, the first time the two have shared a stage.

## One residency that uses the whole city

The most New York booking of the season is not a concert at all. [John Mulaney](/en/concert/en-john-mulaney-takes-manhattan-msg-20270115) stages Mulaney Takes Manhattan as nine nights in nine different rooms, starting January 7 at the Comedy Cellar and climbing through the Blue Note, Joe's Pub, Bowery Ballroom, the Music Box Theatre, the Beacon Theatre, Carnegie Hall and Radio City Music Hall before finishing at Madison Square Garden on January 15, 2027. It works as a map of the city's venues in ascending order — and as a reminder that in New York, the room is half the show.
