Most of what gets written about touring right now is about scale: stadium runs, Sphere residencies, arena tours announced a year out. But a quieter pattern runs underneath the calendar for the next six months. A run of indie and alternative acts, several of them with decades of records behind them, have chosen ballrooms, clubs and chapels over anything bigger. The reasons differ, and they are worth reading closely, because the room tells you what kind of night each band is planning.

Here are six bookings between December 2026 and April 2027 that show what the indie circuit looks like when it stays small on purpose.

## Yo La Tengo: eight nights, no repeats

The template for all of this is [Yo La Tengo's Hanukkah residency](/en/concert/en-yo-la-tengo-hanukkah-new-york-20261204) at the [Bowery Ballroom](/en/venue/Bowery%20Ballroom%2C%20New%20York%2C%20NY), running eight consecutive nights from December 4 to December 11, 2026. Each evening adds a surprise comedian and a surprise musical guest, the band repeats no song across the whole run, and the encore usually turns into covers. Proceeds go to charity.

It is the opposite of a tour. Instead of one set carried from city to city, it is eight different sets in one room. If you only catch one night, you are seeing something nobody else will see again, which is the strongest argument for a small venue there is.

## David Byrne's Night: a tribute that outgrew its pub

The strangest booking of the winter is [David Byrne's Night](/en/artist/David%20Byrne's%20Night), a rotating collective founded by Dundee native Lola Stephen that began as a one-off at Hackney's MOTH Club in 2023. The 2027 UK tour opens at [New Century Hall in Manchester on January 15](/en/concert/en-david-byrnes-night-manchester-20270115) and reaches [Troxy in east London on January 23](/en/concert/en-david-byrnes-night-london-20270123), the largest headline show the project has staged.

The format pairs Talking Heads and David Byrne songs with recitals of Robert Burns poetry, played by a supergroup drawn from English Teacher, The Pogues, The Horrors, Man/Woman/Chainsaw, Goat Girl and others. It lands in the fiftieth-anniversary window of the first Talking Heads album, and it is a good snapshot of how the British indie and punk scene currently entertains itself.

## Dinosaur Jr. open the year at Brooklyn Steel

[Dinosaur Jr.](/en/artist/Dinosaur%20Jr.) start the 2027 leg of their There Near tour with [the first of two nights at Brooklyn Steel](/en/concert/en-dinosaur-jr-there-near-brooklyn-20270211) on February 11. The run then works through Philadelphia, Silver Spring, Carrboro, Florida, Nashville, Chicago, Detroit, Toronto and Boston before finishing on March 13, with Starcrawler supporting most of the dates. A band this loud in a room this size is the whole point: two nights in one venue rather than one night somewhere larger.

## Jake Bugg goes solo, and ends in a church

[Jake Bugg](/en/artist/Jake%20Bugg) releases [Alamo](/en/concert/en-jake-bugg-alamo-20261113), his seventh album, on November 13, 2026. It steers him back toward the folk and country roots of his early records, and the tour follows suit: a stripped-back, ten-date solo run through the UK and Ireland that opens at Albert Hall in Manchester on February 6 and [closes at Union Chapel in London on February 26](/en/concert/en-jake-bugg-alamo-tour-london-20270226). For a singer-songwriter playing alone with nothing to hide behind, a room like that is the right call.

## Beirut's first North American tour since 2019

The most overdue booking on this list is [Beirut](/en/artist/Beirut). Zach Condon opens his first North American tour since 2019 with [two nights at Brooklyn Steel on April 2 and 3](/en/concert/en-beirut-north-american-tour-brooklyn-20270402), supporting A Study of Losses, the band's seventh album. The whole run is only six dates, continuing to Boston, Washington DC, Chicago and Toronto, and general tickets have been on sale since September 11, 2026. Six shows after seven years away is a short window; the New York nights are the obvious ones to aim for.

## Iceage at the Bowery Ballroom

Danish punk band [Iceage](/en/artist/Iceage) bring [their spring tour to the Bowery Ballroom on April 12](/en/concert/en-iceage-north-american-tour-new-york-20270412), one of ten North American shows behind For Love of Grace & the Hereafter, the album they released in May 2026. The run opens April 2 at Neumos in Seattle, comes down the West Coast, crosses the Midwest and finishes April 15 at the Black Cat in Washington, DC. A ten-date run of club shows is exactly the scale this band suits.

## What the small rooms add up to

Put these next to each other and a shape appears. New York's indie spring runs through two venues, Brooklyn Steel and the Bowery Ballroom, and three of the four New York bookings here are consecutive nights rather than one bigger show. London gets a tribute act at Troxy and a solo set at Union Chapel. None of these artists is short of an audience; they are choosing the room that fits the music.

For contrast, the same spring has [Tame Impala opening their UK and European tour at Co-op Live in Manchester on May 28](/en/concert/en-tame-impala-deadbeat-manchester-20270528), and [Supergrass playing the middle night of three at the O2 Forum Kentish Town on May 8](/en/concert/en-supergrass-london-20270508). Both are worth seeing, but they are a different kind of evening.
